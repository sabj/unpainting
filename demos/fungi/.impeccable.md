## Design Context

### Users
People exploring generative art in a browser, and developers inspecting a small open source demo. They choose seeds, inspect mushroom variation, and let forms morph or continuously evolve.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, curious, naturalistic. The user explicitly requested watercolor mushrooms as if in an art book.

### Aesthetic Direction
A natural-history plate on warm paper, with serif specimen names, muted olive controls, and small rust accents. The painting is the focus. A compact one-page studio; no dashboard conventions or external image assets.

### Design Principles
1. Follow recognizable mushroom morphology; keep morphs within one form family.
2. Build vector geometry in pure JavaScript, then paint over it with Unpainting.
3. Preserve stable point correspondence so transitions feel like living forms.
4. Expose seeds and traits without making technical implementation the main experience.
5. Support keyboard controls, narrow screens, reduced motion, and image export.
