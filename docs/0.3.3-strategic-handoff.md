## 2. Root-Cause Diagnosis (Code-Level)

### Gap A: Why Detail Is 4x–10x Coarser Than the Target and Physical Brushes
At `1200 px` width on a `280 mm` sheet (`mmPerCell = 0.2333 mm/cell`), our physical brush kit (`src/paint/brushes.json`) has:
- **`rigger` (#2 liner):** `1.2 mm` wide (`r = 0.6 mm = 2.57 cells`, circular footprint **$\approx 21$ cells** / `1.1 mm²`).
- **`round4` (#4 round):** `3.0 mm` wide (`r = 1.5 mm = 6.43 cells`, circular footprint **$\approx 130$ cells** / `7.1 mm²`).

However, `src/paint/methods/light-to-dark.mjs` applies **five compounded spatial low-pass filters** before any brush is chosen:

1. **`SQUINT` 19x19 pixel (`4.4 mm`, `361 cells`) box blur before value-band allocation (`stepSquint = true`, line 164):**
   - Introduced in commit `523e043b` (did not exist in `0.1.1`, `0.2.1`, `0.3.1` `32b1bfa4`, or `0.3.2` `2d62c356`).
   - Averages `value.values` over a `(2r+1) x (2r+1) = 19 x 19` cell box (`r = Math.round(2 / 0.2333) = 9` cells) *before* computing band weights `weights[0..4]`. Fine darks and lights under `4.4 mm` wide are smeared across bands before shape extraction begins.
2. **Middle-layer `minShape * 4` (`880–1,152 cells`, `~9 mm` blob) area floor & `closeLocal(..., 2)` (line 222):**
   - `const minShape = Math.max(shaped ? 144 : 4, Math.round(w * h * 0.00015));` (`216 cells` at `1200x1200`; `288 cells` at `1200x1600`).
   - On line 222, because `layerSmoothMm` defaults to `2`, the 4 middle layers (`L0..L3`) filter masks with `removeSmall(closeLocal(mask, w, h, 2), w, h, minShape * 4)` — requiring **`864 to 1,152 cells` (`47–63 mm²`, 45x larger than a rigger footprint)**! Every middle-value feature between `21` cells and `~1,000` cells is deleted from the middle layers.
3. **`stepHoles` indiscriminate hole-filling up to `minShape` (`216–288 cells`, line 276):**
   - `if (stepHoles && !step.pig) { const hole = Uint8Array.from(mask, (v, i) => !v && !protectedCells[i] ...); ... }`
   - Fills every lighter gap `< minShape` inside a wash mask unless it is bare white paper (`protectedCells`). Mid-tone lighter features inside a darker surround — such as the Gothic window trefoil cusps and glass reflections on `h36`, sunlit stone blocks on `trumbull`, skin/eye gaps on `mittens`, and sunglasses/facial details on `overlook` — are painted right over by the surrounding dark wash.
4. **Thin-passage discard (`2 * area / perimeter < 4` cells, line 318):**
   - `if (shaped && phase === 'mid' && 2 * list.length / Math.max(1, hard.length + soft.length) < ...) { omittedDemand += total; continue; }`
   - Any middle-value shape with `2 * area / perimeter < 4` cells (`0.93 mm`) is unconditionally discarded. Branching, elongated, or crenellated shapes (window mullions, eyebrows, eyelid creases, tree branches, stone courses) have high perimeter relative to area and are thrown away even when a `#2` rigger or `#4` round brush can fit a continuous stroke path (`shapedPlanePaths`, commit `fcec1eb0`).
5. **Reverted focal detail scaling & small light reserves (`c942cc94`):**
   - Reverting `--detailscale 0.5 --detailseg 1` (with `f3f0e85a`'s grid-free focal mask) and `--smallmarklights 120 --smallisolation 4` stopped `minShape` from shrinking inside focal foreground forms and dropped small enclosed highlights (`mittens` went from 46 reserved small lights to 0, losing the left eye catchlight; `h36` went from 97 reserved small lights to 0).

### Gap B: Why Luminance Is Lost and Orange/Brown Halos Appear (`h34`, `h36`, `trumbull`, `overlook`)
1. **Unpruned minor trace pans + `stepHoles` refilling (`trace-pans.mjs`, commits `9324bfd6` & `f3f0e85a`):**
   - Continuous Kubelka-Munk `unmix()` spreads neutral/stone/shadow absorption across 5–6 pans per cell (`5.02 pans/cell` and `14.52 passes/cell` on `h36`; `4.79 pans/cell` and `15.57 passes/cell` on `h34`).
   - When `stepHoles` fills gaps `< minShape` in a minor warm pan's mask (e.g. burnt sienna or yellow ochre carrying <10% of an area's absorption), it expands that warm pan across adjacent cool/grey cells (`G[i] === 0`), and `TONE.strength` charges it across the whole shape.
   - This lays 15 overlapping glazes per cell instead of ~7.8, darkening the sunlit stone facade on `h34`/`h36`, turning the dog's black coat brown on `overlook`, and creating orange-brown sienna rims around cool grey/green washes.
2. **Benchmark Harness Palette & Plan Omissions in `verify-c942cc94`:**
   - `trumbull` ran without `--paints ochre,rawsienna,hansa,ultramarine,cerulean,sepia,sapgreen` (getting only 4 auto-picked pans: `hansa, ultramarine, rose, sapgreen` — zero earth/ochre pigments for sandstone).
   - `mittens` ran with `--paints sepia,ochre,cadyellow,ultramarine,sapgreen` (missing `sienna` and `umber`).
   - `overlook` ran with no `--valueplan` and no `--paints`.
   - `cma` ran without its line-preparation / `--linemodel strict --linestroke 1 --inkres 2` flags.

### Gap C: Why Ink Either Over-Draws (`dog-ink`) or Over-Prunes (`cma`, `h36`, `h34`)
1. **Missing Auto-Hold on Authored/Plan Ink Files (`dog-ink`):**
   - In `0.3.2` (`harvest/args/dog-ink.args`), `dog-ink` ran with `--inkrecipe v2` (`--linesupport 5 --linesmooth 1 --linesmoothcorner 50 --inkdarkmatch 1 --inkdamp 0.3`).
   - In `verify-c942cc94`, `dog-ink` ran with `--outlinemedium ink --drawingfile .../opening --drawinglast .../closing --washinkfree 1` *without* `--inkrecipe v2`, and `c942cc94` had removed the auto-enable check in `src/paint/methods/ink-lines-file.mjs`. Consequently, `holdInkLines` returned `null` (`flags.measure === false`), drawing all 722 raw contour-map lines unfiltered at full dry concentration while `--washinkfree 1` stripped 5.4% of the sheet out of the wash target.
2. **AggressiveSalience & Length Pruning in `INK_RECIPES.wash` (`cma`, `h36`, `h34`):**
   - Setting `lineminmm: '1.2'`, `inksalience: '1'`, and `inkfoliage: '1'` in `INK_RECIPES.wash` (`src/paint/methods/ink-recipe.mjs`) caused `decideFile` (`src/paint/methods/ink-decide-file.mjs`) to drop short or unclosed pen marks: the lake ripples, figures on the rock, and tree bark ticks in `cma`, and the foliage/masonry pen marks in `h36` and `h34`.

## 3. Select List of Open Technical, Style, and Architecture Questions for the Fresh Session

### Question 1 — Brush-Scale vs. Canvas-Scale Detail Floor (`light-to-dark.mjs`)
Currently, `minShape` scales with canvas pixel area (`w * h * 0.00015` = `216–288 cells` at `1200px`), and middle layers multiply that by `4x` (`minShape * 4` = `864–1,152 cells` when `layerSmoothMm >= 2`). Meanwhile, our physical detail brushes (`rigger` `1.2 mm` = `21 cells`, `round4` `3.0 mm` = `130 cells`) are sized in physical millimetres (`sheetWidthMm / w`).
- **Decision needed:**
  1. Should `minShape` in `planLightToDark` be anchored to **physical brush footprint on the sheet** (e.g., `r_rigger` to `r_round4` in `mm²`, so a `#2` rigger / `#4` round can paint features down to `~25–60 cells` at `1200px`) rather than `w * h * 0.00015`?
  2. Should the middle-layer `minShape * 4` multiplier (`line 222`) be reduced to `minShape` (or scaled progressively across layers `L0 -> L3`, e.g. broad foundation in `L0`, brush-scale detail in `L2..L3`) so later middle layers carry fine stone blocks, window cusps, and facial features?
  3. Should we restore `fcec1eb0` (retaining middle passages with `2 * area / perimeter < 4` whenever `shapedPlanePaths` fits a valid multi-point brush path) and `061950e4` (`fineLayerOwnership` for detail-brush level-set forms)?

### Question 2 — Value-Band Squinting (`stepSquint`) and Hole Filling (`stepHoles`)
Commit `523e043b` coupled `calibratedPlan = true` (which is always on) to `stepSquint = true` (19x19 box blur on `value.values` before band weights) and `stepHoles = true` (filling any non-white hole `< minShape` in every pan mask).
- **Decision needed:**
  1. Should `stepSquint` use a much smaller physical radius (e.g. `0.5–0.8 mm`, ~2–3 cells, matching the rigger radius rather than `layerSmoothMm = 2 mm` = 9 cells), or should band weights be computed from the edge-preserving `smooth` value field (which stops at `contrast >= 0.14` or `colorEdge >= 0.45`) so hard architectural and facial edges are never blurred across bands?
  2. Should `stepHoles` be restricted to **pinholes smaller than the rigger brush footprint** (e.g., `< 16–25 cells` where the target value itself is not a distinct lighter step), and never fill cells where `G[i] === 0` or where a trace pan was pruned?

### Question 3 — Trace-Pan Purity & Luminance (`trace-pans.mjs` / `--pigshare`)
Without trace-pan pruning (`--pigshare 0.1` + `f3f0e85a`), `unmix()` assigns 5–6 pans per cell (~15 passes/cell), muddying sunlit stone luminance on `h34`/`h36` and bleeding brown sienna into black/grey forms on `overlook` and `h34`.
- **Decision needed:**
  1. Should we restore `trace-pans.mjs` (`--pigshare 0.1` with Caratheodory 3-pan refit per cell + `f3f0e85a` hole-fill guard) so each cell uses at most 2–3 pans (`~7.5 passes/cell` instead of `~15`), keeping washes luminous and eliminating warm trace-pan halos?

### Question 4 — Ink Pipeline: Authored Plans vs. Source-Ink Extraction vs. Non-Ink Subjects
We have three distinct ink regimes that currently collide:
1. **True Pen-and-Wash Targets (`h36`, `h34`, `cma`):** Need AI lineart/stroke extraction (`traceLineModel`) + `inkdarkmatch: 1`, **without** `inksalience: 1` or `lineminmm: 1.2` deleting water ripples, figures, and foliage/masonry pen ticks.
2. **Loose Wash-Led Portraits with Sparse Accent Lines (`dog-ink`):** Target (`sourcePenShare = 0.45%` at `1200px`) is 95% dark watercolor washes with sparse outer hair wisps and background wall lines. Running 722 contour-map lines with `--washinkfree 1` destroys the dog's face and hollows out the dark coat. Any `--drawingfile` in ink must automatically run `holdInkLines` (`--linesupport 5 --linesmooth 1 --inkdarkmatch 1`) and drop interior mass-edge contour wires (`--inkdecide 1` / `inMass` filter).
3. **Pure Watercolor / Pencil-Under-Wash Targets (`trumbull`, `mittens`, `overlook`, `dog-paint`, `sargent`):** Must never receive closing ink or contour-map ink wires.

### Question 5 — Core Benchmark Suite & Canonical Plans (`overlook`, `dog-paint`, `dog-ink`, `trumbull`, `mittens`, `h36`, `h34`, `sargent`, `cma`, `homer`)
- **Decision needed:**
  1. Promote **`overlook`** (`1200px`, compared against `0.3.1` System Final `32b1bfa4`) into the primary benchmark cohort alongside `dog-paint`, `dog-ink`, `trumbull`, `mittens`, `h36`, `h34`, `sargent`, `cma`, and `homer` (deprioritizing `hills` and `south-france`).
  2. Freeze one canonical, verified argument/plan manifest for this 10-subject core cohort (ensuring `trumbull` has its sandstone ochre/sienna palette, `mittens` has its sienna/umber portrait palette, `overlook` has an authored/canonical value plan, and `cma` has its line-model flags) so harness flag drift never confounds engine comparisons again.
