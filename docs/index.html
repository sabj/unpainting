How it works

Choose recorded early strokes, carry on a painting, or try a technique. Live runs the recorded strokes through the simulator here. Jump to a thumbnail to continue from that sheet state, or choose Carry on painting. Replay shows the original saved frames without fades, with a labelled stroke path.

Drag to paint. Pinch to zoom, use two fingers to pan. Fit resets the view.

A stylus controls pressure. The round brush has a round cursor; the flat brush shows its width and direction.

Mouse and finger pressure is fixed by default. Turn off “Fixed mouse / finger pressure” for a gentle response to drawing speed. Hold Alt or the stylus barrel button for clean water.

R round · F flat · W water · I ink · P pencil · D dryer · Shift+D dry layer. [ / ] resize. Space pauses drying. Escape exits focus. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes; add Shift to redo.

About the paper & brushes

Water spreads, soaks into the paper and dries. Pigment settles into its texture; dry washes remain transparent layers.

Use a loaded brush for flowing marks, less water for dry brush. The brush reloads with each stroke.

The simulation draws on Curtis et al. (1997), Strassmann (1986) and Kim et al. The physics, the painter and this page are in sabj/unpainting.

Everything runs in this browser: no picture, stroke or painting leaves it. Autosave keeps your sheet in this browser only.
