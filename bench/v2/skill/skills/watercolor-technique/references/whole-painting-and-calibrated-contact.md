# Execution and brush checks

A passage is a wash or group of marks with one job.

A plan names the intended technique. The paper state and brush records show what happened. Review both before changing the next layer.

## Separate useful measurements

- Geometry: selected guide paths, registration (alignment between drawing and reference), gestures converted to brush paths and paint actually laid down must agree about the same landmarks.
- Paper: inspect surface water, absorbed water and how much pigment has set into the paper across the area covered by the stroke and relevant neighbors.
- Brush: amount of paint held in the brush reservoir, available tip water, pigment concentration and delivered material are different quantities.
- Coverage: total pigment increase measures deposited mass; it does not establish a continuous area of color or its visible value.
- Appearance: compare immediate and dried states for edge spread, spots where bare paper shows through, granulation and transparency.

Review the picture as well as the material measurements. Check its intermediate stages even when the finish looks recognizable.

## Inspect the earliest responsible stage

Preserve useful drawing geometry when testing a technique change. Check the opening drawing, broad wash, dried layer and finish against their separate jobs. If a gap or scratch already exists when paint is laid down, first inspect whether the brush touched the paper and released paint; changing a later drying stage may not address it.

Save checkpoints at simulation cell density and record physical dimensions, paper settings, palette, brush load, operation order and source version. A smaller view of the whole picture helps check composition; fine ink and texture need inspection at the simulation's original resolution. Enlarging an export does not add simulation detail.

## Keep comparisons bounded

Change one relevant decision when possible and use the same target, physical sheet and simulation cell density. Distinguish an isolated mark calibration, a study of one passage and a whole-picture comparison. A local image section with a recomputed first wash or a different starting position in the paper texture is a separate experiment.

Performance measurements should include hardware, backend, dimensions and enabled physics options. Report the measured case and its quality review; one faster run does not establish a general speedup.
