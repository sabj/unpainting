Round V5, try {TRY} of 3: a learning round. `bench/feedback/` holds the previous try's painting, its plan files and the
judges' critique.

1. Judge your previous painting yourself first. Before you open `bench/feedback/FEEDBACK.md`, look at your painting and
   the target in `bench/feedback/` and write `bench/SELF-REVIEW.md`: what works, what is wrong, at which stage it went
   wrong, and your own score from 0 to 10 for the finished painting and from 1 to 5 for how plausibly it was painted.
2. Then read `bench/feedback/FEEDBACK.md`. The external judges are noisy: they agree with a human expert in only about
   half of paired comparisons, and the process judge varies by about half a point on identical images. Do not accept
   their notes blindly. In `bench/SELF-REVIEW.md`, add where you agree and disagree with them, and why.
3. Decide what to change from your own judgement and the parts of the critique you agree with. You may keep, change or
   retune anything you control (plan files, recipe tokens, painter settings). Say in `bench/PLAN.md` what you changed and
   why, and which critique points you rejected.
