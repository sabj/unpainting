# V4 and V5 inputs

The model writes a plan for the painter (`tools/paint-plate.mjs`): regions, reserves, styles, drawing paths, recipe tokens and settings. It paints the plan with `bench/paint.sh`; the painter stops at every passage boundary so the model can look and revise what is not yet painted. [round45.mjs](../scripts/pipeline/round45.mjs) runs a round.

- One plan form: `config.json`, recipe tokens over the base `region_w13_lay4_sky3_skyb_cpp`, and painter settings.
- [TASK.md](TASK.md): the brief. [round-V4.md](round-V4.md), [round-V5-first.md](round-V5-first.md) and [round-V5-next.md](round-V5-next.md) fill its round rules; [feedback-section.md](feedback-section.md) is added from V5 try 2.
- [TOKENS.md](TOKENS.md): the recipe tokens and what each sets, generated by [tokens.mjs](../scripts/pipeline/tokens.mjs).
- [PAINT.md](PAINT.md), [paint.sh](paint.sh), [paint.mjs](paint.mjs): the paint commands, each a request file the harness answers.
- [check.sh](check.sh): the admission check ([check.mjs](../scripts/pipeline/check.mjs)).
- [subjects.json](subjects.json): painting width, sheet size, style and drawing medium (`pen`, `pencil` or `none`) of each target.
- [tools/](tools/): the two line tracers the brief offers. [example/](example/): an admitted plan for a synthetic seascape.

Rules:

- One target per session; 85 minutes of the model's own time (time waiting in `bench/paint.sh` is not counted).
- Three looks while painting, at stops the model chooses, and one at the finished painting; a revision follows a look, once. A look shows the target beside the sheet. A strict run (`--notargetlooks 1`) shows the sheet only and removes `bench/plan/target.png` when painting starts. Every answer is kept in `paint-answers.jsonl`. The target guard stays on.
- A finished painting counts. Settings the painter never reads, unrequested options and passages with no brush path are recorded in `pipeline.notes` and `optionCheck`, not failed; `--strict-verdict` fails them, as the published 0.1 results did. A painter killed by a signal, an exit other than 0 or 3, or no finished painting still fail.
- Every subject gets the same base recipe and template. The plan picks its pigments with `--paints`, and they cannot change once painting starts.
- If the session ends without a painting, the harness checks the plan and paints it without stops. A missing or refused plan gets one repair turn in the same session, as does a plan the painter refuses at full size.
- Ink follows the subject's medium. On a subject whose `medium` is not `pen`, any drawing has its opening lines drawn in graphite before the washes, and its closing lines and pen-only settings dropped (`layering.mediumOverride` in the check, `pipeline.mediumOverride` in the try). On a `pen` subject a plan that names no medium is drawn in ink, opening and closing lines; a plan that names one, or draws nothing, runs as written.
- V5 tries after the first get `bench/feedback/`: the previous try's painting, plan, stops and the judges' words. No score or judge number goes back. The model is asked to write `bench/SELF-REVIEW.md` before reading the judges; the order is asked for, not enforced.
