# Example area plan (seascape; not a benchmark subject)

- Value study: three values plus paper. Pale sky, mid sea, dark rock. The cloud is the lightest shape.
- Areas: sky (top 45%), sea (bottom 55%), rock (inside the sea, in front of it).
- Sky: one wet-on-wet wash with a flat 25 mm brush, graded darker at the top. The cloud is soft, so it is lifted from the
  damp wash, not reserved.
- Sea: on dry paper once the sky is dry, so the horizon is hard. A graded wash, darker toward the viewer (ladder values
  1-4), then a dry-brush pass for the darker values (5 and darker), so the paper shows through as broken light.
- Rock: wet on dry, a round 8 mm brush, after the sea's first wash; every ladder value inside it.
- Edges: sky/sea hard (the horizon); sea/rock hard (the rock stands clear).
- Paints: cerulean and ultramarine for sky and sea, sienna and sepia for the rock.
- Drawing: the target has no pen line. A light graphite opening drawing places the horizon and the rock: the sea is
  `scaffold`, the rock `structural`, the sky `none`.
- Failure signs: a hard edge round the cloud, the sky in bands, the sea a flat blue with no broken light.
