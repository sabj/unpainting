# Plan: Hills (aerial oak hillside), loose ink and wash, V4 try 3

## Reference cues
- Aerial view of a hillside: sunlit yellow-green meadow planes (lower right, upper right strip, a wedge at the left), a
  pale peach-ochre dirt path winding down in two visible stretches, and many dark blue-green / navy oak crowns with
  olive and teal mottling. No sky.
- Value thumbnail (4 values): paper-pale path and meadow highlights; light-mid yellow-green meadow; mid olive-teal crowns;
  deep navy-black crowns and trunks (right-centre cluster, lower left, under the big central oak).
- Focal point: the big central oak crown with its dark trunk and branches, where the centre path emerges below it.
- Lights: path crowns (near paper white, peach), pale rim of the top meadow, small white flecks along the lower ridge.
  The painter keeps its own near-paper lights; two small reserves mark the path crowns (category: bare paper, toned
  later by a pale ochre glaze if too white).

## Regions, technique, order
1. Meadows (lower right, top right, left wedge): wash question. One pale connected yellow-green wash per meadow, wet on
   dry, graded lighter toward the sunlit upper edges; then a mid-green plane on dry paper where the slope turns; a
   shadow glaze crosses meadow into the tree edge. `precise-watercolor` per region (more washes, little ink). Nothing
   stippled; the mottling in the target is texture, left to paper grain and the mid layers.
2. Paths (left, centre): one pale peach-ochre wash, wet on dry, kept clean; the long edges are carried by the pen, not by
   hard paint outlines. `precise-watercolor`.
3. Woodland (fallback family over the whole sheet plus right, left, top, central oak, lower-right oak): `ink-and-wash`.
   Mixed-dark connected masses (blue, green, rose-violet shadow, not ink black): pale olive-green base crowns, dry,
   mid blue-green on dry paper, dry, deep navy shadow glaze across crowns and over the meadow edge; trunks, branches
   and ridge crests last in pen.
4. Drawing: opening = 8 sparse pen lines (path edges, three ridge crests), long single gestures drawn before the washes.
   Closing = 22 lines: the same edges lighter, plus trunks and branches of the central oak, the tree at top right,
   the lower-right oak. No ink in the canopy interiors (the stock tracer only finds scribble there) and none in the
   meadows. Ink pigment: base sepia (`--inkk` from `_sep8`), checked in the pigment log.

## Moisture, load, edges
- Meadow and path washes: medium load, watery enough to flow, on dry paper; edges soft only where meadow meets lit path.
- Crowns: medium-thick paint on dry paper for mid values (crisp, irregular edges); deepest navy charged in one connected
  group so the dark masses have value steps.
- Edges: hard along the ridge crests and path rims (pen reinforces), lost inside the dark woodland.

## Tokens and settings

- Changes after looking are recorded under "Revisions" below.

## What would show failure
- Meadows blotchy or striped, or dead-flat; path greyed or covered by canopy wash.
- Canopies turning into one black mass without value steps, or scribble ink over blurry paint.
- Pen lines that look ruled, or ink visible in the lit meadows.
- Seams: straight region edges where the target has none.
