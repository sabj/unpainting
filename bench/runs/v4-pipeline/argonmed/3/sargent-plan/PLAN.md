## 2. Style, Palette & Value-Plan Strategy (Revised after Self-View Looks 1–3)
- **Pure Watercolor, No Ink**: Both `opening-lines.json` and `closing-lines.json` specify `"lines": [], "medium": "none"`, ensuring zero pen/ink strokes are drawn while all structure, dark accents, and calligraphy come purely from watercolor brush passes, value layers, dark accents, and chromatic touchpoints.
- **Warm Cream Paper Base (`--paperrgb 221,215,199`)**: `estimatePaper` identifies the sunlit sail region at `221/215/199`, which does not touch the crop border because the upper-right spars frame the corner (causing `--paperrgb auto` to fall back to `252,251,248`). Explicitly setting `--paperrgb 221,215,199` matches the target's warm cream paper ground and improves the palette unmixing error from `3.18` down to `2.52` (`hansa yellow`, `phthalo blue`, `quinacridone rose`, `sap green`, `French ultramarine`, `Payne's grey`, plus granulating sky-blue `French ultramarine`).
- **Recipe (`region_w13_rg0_acc8_dp85_dg2_dk14`)**:
  - `rg0` (`--groundglaze 0`) disables the architectural ground-glaze split at `y = 0.62`, eliminating the horizontal step across the middle of the central sail seen in Look 2 and Look 3.
  - `acc8_dp85_dg2_dk14` (`--accentshare 0.8 --deepaccent 85 --deepgrow 2 --darkgain 1.4`) preserves small dark features (pulley blocks, sail rim, reef-point shadows, foot-of-sail shadow) and restates deep darks in bands 4 and 5 on dry paper, followed by two chromatic touchpoint rounds (`tp2`).
- **Paper Reserves**: Explicit reserves are kept strictly inside the sunlit upper-right sail (`upper-right-sky-light` and `mid-right-sail-gap`), while interior creases and glints are reserved organically by `paperLights` and `paperRel` without rectangular cutouts.
