## Moisture and glazes

Before every pigment or pen stroke the fixed executor must measure the entire footprint and neighboring halo. A dry-paper passage starts only after measured dryness; already-wet contacts are permitted only as deliberate overlap within that same connected passage. Otherwise wait/dry before continuing. Do not trust elapsed time or invent a reading. Use normal Fast-mode drying and real pigment deposition, no physics suppression, image overlays or export recoloring.

The connected shadow support ignores family ownership. Planned glazes cross (a) shaded house wall, eave/recess and nearby garden, (b) neighboring olive crowns into their ground shadow, and (c) foreground crown/trunk-root into earth. Urban style redistributes shadow share into the pale base outside the actual shadow support; this is appropriate to the pen sketch. No wet-in-wet break is requested: the reference's crisp architectural edges and urban style call for dry placement and own-passage merging only. Palette: ochre, burnt sienna, hansa, ultramarine, sepia, sap green. Greens are tempered with earths and blue, and structural ink stays neutral black. Raw-sienna cues above are mixed with ochre and sienna; cerulean cues use diluted ultramarine.

4. After light-color passes: architecture stays light; olive masses surround but do not swallow the focal houses. Cypresses are tall and tapered. Check value shapes before more shadows.
5. Before closing drawing: lights intact; restrained connected shadow shapes; no blurry focal walls. Revise only future work if a specific visible defect requires it. Do not add heavy closing ink to rescue bad wash edges.
6. Finish: inspect thumbnail values, protected lights, hard/soft/lost edges, roof/window/trunk landmarks, purposeful marks and light-to-dark progress. Then stop.

Failure signs: a near-blank sketch, dark all-over wall fill, blurred house edges, disconnected foliage chips, excessive green saturation, repeated parallel strokes, black canopy outlines, or an evenly traced drawing everywhere. The allowed whole-painting checkpoints are the fast proof in this frozen task; no separate physics test is permitted.
