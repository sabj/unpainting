## Glazes and paper checks

The left-chimney/wing glaze crosses stone cells, openings and eave divisions; the recess interiors retain their own fitted mixture. The gable-side glaze connects into the lower wall and arches without filling the lit facade. The right cornice glaze crosses the bay wall, window surrounds and side return. The ground glaze crosses pavement and feet. All begin on a fully dry base. The architecture mixture is ultramarine plus raw sienna, as the green base palette lacks burnt sienna; the fixed `shs` control prevents pure-blue chimney shadows.

Before every stroke the executor must check measured moisture over its complete footprint and neighbors. For dry glazes and focal details, wait or work elsewhere if the base is wet. Same-passage wet overlap is allowed for broad washes; it must not spill into dry fine details. Water amount and pigment concentration are separate: dilute pigment for the foundation, medium for planes and glazes, stronger controlled paint for recess accents. Do not lower water globally to make walls scratchy. No blooms into a damp layer; no overlay or export recoloring.

The narrow sky gaps are placed wet on dry to keep steep architectural edges controlled; fresh overlaps within each connected wash stay open. This is the named departure from prewetting the whole sky. There is no dark-first departure.
