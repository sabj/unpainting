## Values and style

Five-value thumbnail: (1) white background, white bib, muzzle, forehead and nose ridge; (2) pale warm skin and cream fur; (3) ochre tabby stripes, iris bodies and soft cheek turns; (4) connected charcoal hair, brows and the cat's shaded flank; (5) very small pupils, upper lids, nostrils and mouth corners. The painting progresses from a sparse placing drawing through pale foundations, two middle wash layers, then dry shadow glazes and selective closing lines. The dark hair must build gradually and remain paint.

Use precise-watercolor for all fitted value planes. The fur and curls pose an ink-and-wash resolution question, while their underlying masses pose a wash question. Families label reference cues and passage choices; they are overlapping descriptive bounds, not rectangular paint shapes. Every family uses the same style, so no rectangular style seam is introduced.

## Checkpoints and failure criteria

Before physics, run `bash bench/check.sh` and inspect the compiled family/style assignments, reserves and passage intents. The admission step is the available planning proof; do not launch a separate physics render.

1. Opening stop: a light, sparse recognizable placing sketch with both pairs of eyes and proportionate features. Failure: missing face, full hair ink fill, heavy lineart, displaced lids.
2. Light washes: pale connected local colors, hair still light grey, white bib and face lights intact. Judge as a foundation, not a finished portrait. Failure: scratches, fragmented paint islands, clipped rectangular edges, spills into catchlights.
3. Middle values: tabby pattern and cheek planes readable, hair connected and darker, broad paper lights still clear. Failure: blurred eyes, isolated equal stamps, repetitive stripe contours, muddy complexion.
4. Shadow/closing boundary: values coherent across cheek/neck and hair/temple; focal features firm, drawing subordinate to paint. Failure: ink used to rescue blurry paint, feature blobs, pale hair with black pen fill.
5. Finished: compare thumbnail value shapes first, then lights, hard/soft/lost edges, eye spacing/nose/mouth/ear set, purposeful tapered marks, and credible light-to-dark progress. No acceptance from pixel-error scores.

Record any self-view revision here with the observed failure, the pending passage change and its reason. Once painting finishes, stop.
