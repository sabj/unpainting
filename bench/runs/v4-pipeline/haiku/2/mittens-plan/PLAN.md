# Painting Plan: Mittens, V4, Try 2

## Value Thumbnail
Three values: lights (white paper, catchlights, pale skin and fur), mid-tones (skin planes, ear structure, whisker guides), shadows (face shadow-side, eye sockets, ear shading, cat's dark markings).

Focal point: both faces, especially the eyes where likeness lives.

## Lights Reserved
- Both person's eyes: catchlights in each eye (upper inner corner), upper eyelid highlights
- Person's nose ridge lit edge and lip highlights
- Cat's eyes: catchlights in each eye
- Cat's muzzle: palest whisker areas and cheek highlights
- White paper background: vignette edge

## Per-Region Technique

### Person (face, neck, hair)
**Reference cue:** Light portrait with fine lines and soft washes; person looking right with dark hair on left side, lighter face on right side.

**Pale skin wash (wet on evenly damp paper):** 
- One connected passage over the face and neck
- Keep eyes dry or barely tinted
- Motion: big brush, transparent, few strokes
- Paper state: damp with shine mostly gone
- Water: watery to medium
- Pigment: pale ochre or skin tone mix

**Mid planes (dry on dry paper):**
- Eye sockets (hard edge on upper lid, soft on lower)
- Side of nose (one side hard, one soft)
- Under-lip shadow and jaw turn
- Cheekbone definition on shadow side
- Motion: controlled, pointed round, one stroke per plane
- Paper state: fully dry
- Water: medium load
- Pigment: medium value, same family as wash

**Iris and eyes (dry on dry paper):**
- Target-sized disc tucked under upper lid
- Catchlight left out of the iris
- Sclera as pale tint (not bare white, not skin color)
- Upper lid as dark ribbon, thicker at outer corner, tapered
- Lower lid as light line, not a crease
- Motion: precise, small round brush
- Paper state: fully dry

**Shadow glaze (wet on dry, cross-boundary):**
- One connected glaze across the shaded side of the face
- Flows from cheek into neck and collar
- Into the dark hair area
- One glaze unifies multiple regions
- Paper state: fully dry
- Water: watery to medium
- Pigment: cool shadow tone

**Hair (ink and wash):**
- Dark mass painted in mixed dark (not pure black), broken tones
- Value wash first, then shadow glaze
- Closing ink: gestural directional strokes following growth direction
- Density follows value; none in the lights

**Face closing ink (selective, on dry paint):**
- Lid lines light and selective
- Pupil dark center
- Nostril
- Mouth corner only
- Pigment: sepia or neutral black

### Cat (head, ears, body)
**Reference cue:** Tabby cat face looking upward, orange and brown fur with darker markings, alert expression.

**Fur mapping:**
- Head mass: lit on top and right, shadow on left
- Ear locks: long, tapered, lit edges reserved
- Muzzle: blue-grey sheen, short fur
- Cheeks and jaw: curved masses

**Value wash (wet on dry or barely damp, one connected passage):**
- Mixed dark browns: orange, sienna, ultramarine, hint of black (not pure black)
- Lit side paler (add white to the mix)
- Shadow side stronger
- Motion: big brush, connected passage, one pass per mass
- Paper state: dry to barely damp
- Water: medium to slightly watery
- Pigment: medium to thick

**Shadow glaze (wet on dry, cross-boundary into person's shoulder area):**
- Across head and into person's shoulder if they overlap
- Dark side of face and neck
- Into ear shadow areas
- One glaze unifies cat and person where they meet
- Paper state: fully dry

**Directional strokes (dry on dry, following growth):**
- Long ribbon strokes for ear locks
- Short curved strokes on muzzle (S and C curves for curls)
- Density follows value; none in the lights
- Gestural scribble acceptable, ruled hatch not acceptable
- Pigment: darker value of fur color, or mixed dark

**Eyes (dry on dry paper):**
- Small catchlights reserved in both eyes
- Iris tucked under lid
- Round pupils with intensity
- Sclera pale tint
- Upper lid as dark line
- Motion: precise, small brush
- Paper state: fully dry

**Whiskers and muzzle detail (closing ink, dry on dry):**
- Fine whisker lines
- Nose shape and nostrils
- Mouth area
- Gestural, following natural whisker growth

**Closes:** Selective dark accents in the valleys between locks.

## Drawing Plan

**Opening lines (pen/pencil before washes):**
- Sparse and confident
- Person: eye placement, brow arc, nose profile, mouth corners, ear placement, jaw line
- Cat: eye placement, ear outline, muzzle contour, cheek bulges, whisker guides
- Long connected gestures on one dip, painter's-first-lines style

**Closing ink (last, selective toning on dry paint):**
- Eye details: pupils, lid lines, lashes where focal
- Nostril, mouth corners
- Whiskers in foreground (cat)
- Contours where they explain form without filling

## Order (Light to Dark)

1. Opening pen drawing (sparse structure)
2. Reserve lights (catchlights, glints, white paper)
3. Pale skin wash (connected passage, person's face and neck)
4. Dry
5. Pale fur wash (connected passage, cat's head and body, mixed dark browns)
6. Dry
7. Mid-value planes on dry paper (eye sockets, nose sides, jaw, ear structure)
8. Dry
9. Eyes on dry paper (iris, lid, sclera, pupils light)
10. Shadow glaze across face-side (unifies person face shading)
11. Dry
12. Shadow glaze across cat head and into overlap (unifies cat and connection)
13. Dry
14. Directional strokes for fur texture (gestural, following growth)
15. Dry
16. Closing ink selective: pupils, lid lines, whiskers, nostril, mouth corners
17. Lift any over-bright reserved lights if needed and tone with pale glaze

## Edge Plan

- Hard edges: upper eyelids, iris outline, nose profile, mouth corners, ear outer contours
- Soft edges: cheekbone turn, jaw line to neck, transition from lit to shadow on cat's head, where flesh turns away
- Lost edges: where shadow merges into background (hair into background, ear edges into shoulder)

## What Would Show Failure

- Blur at eyes and whiskers (detail painted wet or in wet passage) → fix by painting eyes on fully dry paper
- Over-bright paper whites blazing without toning → fix by selective pale glaze on reserved lights
- Patchwork from region-by-region fills → use cross-boundary shadow glaze instead
- Heavy ink with heavy blur (detail muddy) → paint detail dry first with controlled edges
- Muddy mixed darks from too many pigments → stick to ochre, sienna, ultramarine, sepia, white
- Cat fur as uniform stipple or tiled patches → use directional strokes following growth, gestural scribble

## Painter Settings

Recipe: Base V096 + tokens to control layers and drying.
- Light washes early, shadow glazes late
- Detail at eyes and whiskers on dry paper
- Fine lines and light washes per task request

Expected painting duration: 25-45 passes (light to dark ladder).
