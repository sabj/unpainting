# Painting plan: Hills, loose ink and wash

- Reference cues: rolling hills with bright yellow-green grass slopes, dark forested areas, a winding path through the landscape, pale hazy sky
- Style: loose ink and wash—value washes, shadow glazes, then directional ink marks
- Approach: let the painter's natural segmentation find the sky, grass, and forest regions; no explicit families to avoid seams
- Protected lights: painter's own near-paper light detection (brightest slopes and path)
- Drawing: opening lines trace the winding path; closing ink adds selective accents along landscape forms following growth and structure
- Values: light to dark layering—pale sky, light grass, mid values for forest structure, dark shadow glazes for depth
- Tokens: `lay2` (two mid-value layers for efficient coverage), `ink4` (full closing ink pass for landscape detail)
- Failure signs: muddy grass, blurry path, lost bright slopes, flat forest, heavy uniform ink
