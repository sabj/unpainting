# Painting Plan: South France Urban Sketch, Try 3

## Style: urban sketch, pen plus light wash
Reference: Tuscan hillside with terracotta buildings, cypress trees, green fields and soft distant hills.

## 1. Value thumbnail
- **Lights**: bright sky, sunlit building wall (the eastern face), field light patch
- **Halftones**: distant hills, building shadow sides, cypress foliage
- **Darks**: tree silhouettes, building recesses, deep shadows in folds
- **Focal point**: main buildings at mid-right, where pen detail and warm terracotta draw the eye
- **Progression**: pale sky and background, mid-value local colors, shadows last

## 2. Reserves (lights protected before wash)
- Sky bright edge (top-left rim)
- Building sunlit terracotta wall (warm plane catching light)
- Field light patch (pale grass area, foreground interest)
- Sky gaps between distant tree masses

## 3. Per-region technique

### Sky (top 35%, soft atmospheric)
- **Reference cue**: pale blue-grey sky, even soft light
- **Resolution**: wash question—the sky reads as a plane, not texture
- **Move**: wet in wet, one passage connecting the whole upper region
- **Paper state**: glossy wet, free merging
- **Load**: watery, pale blue-grey, minimal pigment
- **Edge roles**: soft all around; sky meets distant hills on one soft edge, meets landscape on one soft edge
- **Layer**: one pale wash only; no further texture

### Distant hills (soft greyed masses, 25–45% of height)
- **Reference cue**: soft receding green-brown hillsides, no detail, soft shadows
- **Resolution**: wash question—distance, soft transition
- **Move**: wet on dry (after sky dries), charged into damp paper at the horizon edge
- **Paper state**: damp with sheen, controlled soft blending
- **Load**: medium, greyed green (ultramarine + hansa + touch of sienna)
- **Edge roles**: soft top (sky merge), soft bottom (fade into field)
- **Layer**: pale local color wash; shadow glaze later if needed

### Buildings (mid-right 35–75% vertical, 50–100% horizontal)
- **Reference cue**: terracotta forms, warm ochre, hard architectural edges, some shadow sides
- **Resolution**: ink-and-wash question—pen lines carry structure, wash gives local color
- **Move**: wet on dry
- **Paper state**: dry
- **Load**: medium, warm ochre and sienna local color wash
- **Edge roles**: hard edges where building meets sky or landscape; soft edge on shadow side
- **Layer sequence**:
  1. Opening pen: sparse lines for roof lines, visible wall planes, window openings, junctions
  2. Pale local color wash (ochre/sienna mix, warm, lit side fuller value)
  3. Shadow glaze across the shaded face (one connected glaze crossing walls, not separate fills)
  4. Closing ink: selective dark accents in windows, door recesses, shadow corners only

### Landscape / field (bottom 65–100%)
- **Reference cue**: yellow-green sloped terrain, cast shadows from buildings and trees, light patches
- **Resolution**: wash question—planes and shadow shapes, not texture or individual grass blades
- **Move**: wet on dry
- **Paper state**: dry
- **Load**: pale, medium—warm yellow-green local color for lit fields, cooler for shadow
- **Edge roles**: field edges soft where it meets sky, hard where path or feature creates line
- **Layer sequence**:
  1. Pale yellow-green wash over the field, grading toward the distance (paler, greyer)
  2. Shadow glaze one connected passage across areas where terrain turns or cast shadow falls
  3. No texture marks; field reads as plane

### Trees / cypress accents (scattered, mostly left side and around buildings)
- **Reference cue**: dark vertical cypress silhouettes, green rounded foliage clumps
- **Resolution**: ink-and-wash question—vertical directional pen work and green wash, not individual brushstrokes
- **Move**: wet on dry
- **Paper state**: dry
- **Load**: dark green (ultramarine + hansa), medium consistency for wash
- **Edge roles**: hard vertical silhouette against sky or field; soft edge where foliage clumps overlap
- **Layer sequence**:
  1. Opening pen: tall thin cypress profiles, tree mass outlines
  2. Green-dark wash over foliage clumps (not full-strength; paler on sunlit edge)
  3. Closing ink: selective dark accents at edges only, following natural growth direction

## 4. Glaze list (shadow passes)
- **Building shadow face**: one connected glaze from the building's lit side into its shadow side, crossing where the form turns. Cool, transparent, one hard edge on the lit side.
- **Field shadow glaze**: one connected passage across the bottom field where terrain turns or where a building casts shadow. Cooler, slightly darker, soft merging.
- **Tree shadows**: absorbed into tree wash; accent ink only if needed.

## 5. Wet passages and dry barriers
- Sky is the only full wet-in-wet passage. Everything else waits for sky to dry.
- Distant hills charged into damp paper at the horizon, then allowed to dry fully before landscape wash.
- Buildings and landscape stay separate (dry boundaries where they meet).
- Trees painted on dry paper after landscape dries.

## 6. Drawing
- **Opening paths**: sparse, confident pen lines for building structure (roof lines, wall planes, openings), cypress silhouettes, path edges. No fill, no hatch.
- **Closing ink**: selective dark accents at focal points only—window recesses, dark corners, shadow edges where they need definition. Light on lit areas; never in reserved lights.
- **Ink pigment**: neutral sepia (warm) or black, declared in material profile.

## 7. Breaks from light-to-dark
- None. The plan follows the default sequence: pale sky wash, local colors, shadow glazes last.

## 8. Stage intents
- **After opening pen**: sparse architectural drawing visible, no wash yet
- **After sky wash**: soft pale sky, ready for landscape; sky looks atmospheric, not detailed
- **After local color washes**: buildings have warm terracotta tone, field is pale yellow-green, hills are soft grey-green; no shadows yet, very light overall
- **After shadow glaze**: depth appears; buildings show form; field shows terrain slope
- **After closing ink**: structure and definition at focal points; detail restraint in background; sketch-like quality maintained

## 9. Recipe changes
- Base: `region_w13_lay4_ink4_rec2x_rcl_sky3_leaf_sep8_cg13_vio_rg10_blk60_bmm40_bka20_birr_skyb_cpp_dk13_tv8_uncap_pgreen_dwg200_tp2_shs`

- Reason: urban sketch style calls for lighter washes, less mid-tone layering; detail comes from pen, not accumulation of washes
- Config: two simple regions (sky 0-0.35, landscape 0.34-1) with empty reserves, letting painter preserve lights naturally
- Pigments: rawsienna, sienna, hansa, ultramarine, cerulean, sepia (warm palette for Tuscan terracotta and green)

## Notes
- No texture marks; all form and depth from value shapes, edge placement, and pen structure
- Reserve lights are modest (sky rim, building wall, field patch); not aggressively bright
- Ink weight restraint: pen adds definition at focal points only, not throughout
- Paper showing in reserved whites and untouched areas reinforces sketch aesthetic
