# Painting Plan — Hills (Loose Ink and Wash)

## 1. Visual Analysis of Target (`bench/plan/target.png`)
- **Subject & Composition**: A loose ink-and-wash landscape (`306×242` px, rendered on a `1224×968` cold-press sheet at `158.05 mm` width) showing sunlit yellow-green rolling hills cut by a winding pale warm sandy-cream dirt road. Dense groves of olive-green and cool blue-slate/indigo trees climb the left hillside, sweep diagonally from upper-left to lower-right across the central valley, and cluster along the right edge.
- **Light & Paper**: The brightest passages are the warm cream winding road (`R≈238–247, G≈217–231, B≈138–192`) entering from the bottom center (`x≈0.44–0.56, y≈0.58–0.96`), curving through the mid-left clearing (`x≈0.16–0.28, y≈0.16–0.52`), and reappearing at the upper-right crest (`x≈0.70–0.77, y≈0.00–0.10`) and lower-right corner (`x≈0.71–0.84, y≈0.93–1.00`). Source-relative paper estimation (`_skyb` / `--paperrel 1`) preserves these brightest road and clearing passages organically without axis-aligned box seams.
- **Colour & Palette**: Fitted wide box palette (`_cpp`) selects **Hansa Yellow**, **Sap Green**, **Cerulean Blue**, and **Quinacridone Rose** (plus dark ink), reaching the bright yellow-green sunlit grass (`hansa yellow + sap green`), warm road buffs (`hansa yellow + quinacridone rose`), mid-tone olive canopies (`sap green + hansa yellow + quinacridone rose`), and cool blue-slate tree shadows (`cerulean blue + sap green + quinacridone rose`).
- **Ink Linework**: Expressive dark pen lines define the winding road edges, hill contours, rounded foliage clumps, and tree trunks/branches. Both an opening structural drawing (`opening-lines.json`, 350 contours) and a full closing ink pass over dried washes (`closingink4-lines.json`, 735 contrast-verified lines) are used.

## 2. Passage Schedule (Light to Dark, `region_w13_spl_lad_tp3`)
1. **Opening Drawing (`opening-lines.json`)**: Structural pen contours (`>= 4.5 mm`) establishing the road boundaries, hill ridges, and primary tree canopy silhouettes before washes begin.
2. **Band 1 — Pale Foundation Washes (`_w13`, `_spl`, `_lad`)**: Pale foundation washes laying warm yellow-green across the sunlit hills and soft cool undertones under the wooded slopes while preserving the bare road highlights.
3. **Band 2 — Four Progressive Dry Middle Layers (`_lay4`, L0–L3, `_spl`, `_lad`)**: Four value-stepped wet-on-dry middle layers using adaptive multi-lane stroke planning (`--strokeplan 1 --lanemm 6 --laneadapt 1`) to build the sunlit grass slopes into richer sap-green and olive foliage masses and cool cerulean-slate middle tones (`_cg13`, `_rg10`).
4. **Bands 4 & 5 — Connected Shadow Glazes (`_dk13`, `_shs`) and Three Touch-Up Rounds (`_tp3`, `--touchgain 0.8`)**: Coherent cross-region shadow glazes over dry paint (`--darkgain 1.3`, `--shadowraw 1`) deepening the blue-slate and dark olive-indigo hollows inside the tree groves, followed by three rounds of shape-average corrective glazes (`--touchpoints 3 --touchgain 0.8`) across all 24 colour/density families so the upper-right and lower-right sunlit hillsides and foliage masses reach full target saturation and value.
5. **Closing Ink (`closingink4-lines.json`, `_ink4`, `_uncap`, `_dwg200`)**: Crisp dark pen linework (`735` lines) over the dried washes to restate tree canopies, interior foliage curls, trunks, and road edge accents.
