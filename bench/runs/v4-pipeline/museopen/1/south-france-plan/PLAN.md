# France — South France Village — Urban Sketch, Pen plus Light Wash

## Reference cues
- Provençal hillside village: terracotta roofs (warm ochre/sienna), stone walls, dense cypress and olive foliage, foreground sunlit tree.
- Light is bright, high, slightly warm. Dominant white paper, pale flat washes. Pen structure carries form.
- No dramatic sky; background is distant hills and far houses, paler and cooler.

## Regions and technique
- **Houses** [0.35,0,0.95,0.65] — wet on dry, crisp pen junctions for roofs/walls, warm ochre/sienna flat wash, terracotta roofs slightly darker.
- **Foliage-mid** [0,0.25,1,0.85] — wet on dry, broken washes, varied greens (sap green + ochre + ultramarine), dry brush for texture, cypress verticals in pen.
- **Foreground** [0,0.55,0.65,1] — wet on dry, foreground tree with reserved sunlit edge, light olive/ sap green wash, trunk in sepia/umber pen.
- **Background** [0,0,1,0.35] — wet on dry, distant hills paler cooler (cerulean + ochre), far houses hinted with minimal pen.

Order: opening pen first (traced hard lines), then light-to-dark washes (pale ochre/sienna/green, then slightly deeper roof/ shadow glazes), then selective closing pen to reinforce junctions and cypress/tree structure.

## Moisture and load
- Urban-sketch: per-layer calibrated load, water 0.5 across, paintStrength 0.25 — very light washes, dominant white.
- Flat brush and small round, wet on dry, glazed moves. Avoid wet-in-wet blooms; keep washes flat and pale.

## Protected lights
- Paper whites kept everywhere (urban-sketch reserve: white paper majority, trim, sky gaps, lit walls).
- Explicit reserves: lit wall on right cluster, foreground tree sunlit edge, distant lit wall upper, roof highlight. Painter also keeps near-paper lights.

## Edges and values
- Edges: confident connected pen junctions; pale flat washes; soft lost edges only in distant foliage.
- Values: light-to-mid only; no deep blacks. Darkgain 1.1 (lighter than base 1.3), 2 mid layers (lay2) for few pale washes.
- Ink: sepia ink (0.396,0.481,0.619) scale 4, opening + closing pen (ink4), drawwidthgain 2 for confident lines.

## What would show failure
- Over-dark washes losing white paper; muddy greens; missing pen structure (roofs/cypress not readable); banded sky; lost terracotta warmth.

## Pigments
- ochre, raw sienna, sienna, ultramarine, cerulean, sap green, umber, sepia — warm Provençal palette, mixed on paper for varied greens and stone.
