# Ships — Sargent harbour study (pure watercolor, no ink)

- Reference cues: large central blue-grey hull dominates, granulating wash with warm rust rivets and dark porthole; anchor and keel shadow below with ochre and deep indigo; right mast with dotted washes and blue shadow; pale sky and turquoise sea with reserved paper.
- Regions: hull (centre, precise washes), foreground/anchor (lower, dry-brush), mast-right (vertical dotted forms), sky (top, wet-in-wet soft). All precise-watercolor; sky described as wet-in-wet for soft horizon.
- Protected lights: small hull highlight streaks and anchor sparkle as reserves; main paper whites are the painter's own near-paper lights (sky, hull streaks, sea).
- Edges: crisp where hull meets sky/sea, soft turning edge on hull, lost edges on mast shadows and distant water.
- Values: light-to-dark ladder; mid-gain lifted for pale Sargent palette, soft arcs for granulation, feathered loads.
- Tokens: _soft (granulating soft edges), _ew5 (edge wobble for hand), _mg12 (mid gain), _lf (load feather), _sg12 (style gain).
- Failure: flat blue hull without rivet warmth, lost anchor, sky banding, heavy darks.
