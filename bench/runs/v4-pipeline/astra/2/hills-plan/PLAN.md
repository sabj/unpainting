## Regions and execution order

All first colour goes wet on dry with a watery-to-medium flowing load (profile water 0.55). Adjacent strokes in the same connected passage may merge while open. Dry fully before the shadow stage, then use a medium pigment load with less water (profile 0.40), keeping one selected crisp edge and losing the internal crown transitions. Distant masses stay quieter through fitted colour/value, rather than through indiscriminate blur.

Pale painted planes: all three cream paths and the sunlit grass; do not reserve whole tracks as white. Negative-painted gaps: the reference-derived light spaces between irregular canopies. No rectangular manual reserves, masked lines, or planned lifts are needed. Path warmth enters in the light wash, not as an attempt to paint light over dark later.

For every stroke the executor must check measured paper moisture over the actual footprint and neighbours. Opening/closing ink require fully dry paper.
