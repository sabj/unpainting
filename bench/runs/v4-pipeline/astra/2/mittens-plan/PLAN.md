# Mittens — light washes and fine sepia lines

Reference: `bench/targets/mittens-crop.png`, 300 × 259 source pixels, painted at 1200 × 1036 on a 234.1068 mm sheet. One source pixel spans four native cells; one native cell is 0.19509 mm. This independent plan uses only the supplied reference and fixed painter. The watercolor-technique and painting-planner guides inform the choices below.

## Value thumbnail and composition

The cat occupies the left half, looking up, its ears reaching the top. The man's head enters from the lower right. Their contact edge is the principal shared dark. Five values: untouched paper in the background/chest/muzzle/forehead; pale cream on skin and ear interiors; warm amber tabby and skin half-tones; medium brown cheek stripes/brows/beard; charcoal curls and small focal eye/nostril accents. The cat's large near eye is the first focal point; the man's eyes are the secondary focus. The painting must retain generous white paper.

All region boxes are descriptive ownership cues. The painter retains the target-derived irregular geometry, with precise-watercolor for all regions. No style boundary cuts a face or hair mass. All pigment starts wet on dry; no intentional wet-in-wet passage, because this reference has crisp broken edges and light sketch washes.

At this density, a wash body should be at least four cells (0.78 mm) across. W13's fitted adaptive brush paths retain their native radii; those radii are inspected in the compile instructions rather than replaced with a guessed global brush. A 2–5 cell lid is comparable to or smaller than a useful brush diameter, so its thin structure belongs to pen. Widths in the drawing files are 0.34–0.70 source pixels, tapered along the path, about 1.36–2.8 native pixels before nib calibration. The compiled brush radii and support-width estimates are the executable evidence; no resolution increase or upscaled preview is claimed as new detail.

Four explicit small catchlight reserves, each at least 1 mm across, protect both cat eyes and both human eyes. The background, cat muzzle/chest, ear interiors, forehead, nose ridge and lip sheen use the painter's target-derived paper-light mask; they are not large rectangular reserves. Iris and scleral light planes receive pale local paint where the reference carries tone. The nose and lips have broad pale planes rather than invented bright dots. White whiskers are negative gaps in the washes. No lifting or opaque white is planned.

Opening drawing: 33 curated paths, chiefly fitted ear edges, eyes, nose/mouth, selected long whiskers, contact contour and a few curl edges. The source trace is selected for structure and narrowed; a few long ear/forehead gestures follow visible reference lines. Closing: 23 selective fitted paths; no full silhouette restatement, no fur fill, no regular hatching. Material is explicitly sepia-ink, with sepia absorption coefficients and scale 4. ## Paper state and glaze order

1. Protect paper lights and place the opening pen on dry paper.
2. Pale connected warm/neutral bases; watery pigment with the fixed calibrated wash water load (0.62). Neighbouring strokes may join their own open edge, but eye detail stays separate.
3. Dry the whole footprint and neighbours. Two middle-value layers with controlled medium pigment on dry paper, not a stack of four nested contour levels.
4. Dry, then shared shadow glazes (calibrated water 0.45, medium pigment): the cat's right cheek into shoulder/contact edge; the hairline and temple into the man's cheek/jaw; the nose base into moustache/lip. These follow connected shadow support across descriptive regions, not separate boxed fills. Retain the executor's two final shadow stages, with darkgain 1.1.
5. Dry and inspect; small closing sepia accents if still appropriate.

The fixed executor measures primary contact plus neighbouring paper before every value-plan stroke. Its dry-paper gate dries and remeasures a non-dry footprint before a dry move; its own-wet-edge allowance connects the current wash. Retain these checks and the target guard. Never infer moisture from elapsed time. Drawing also uses the physical material model on the dry sheet.

## Changes from the fixed base and acceptance checks

Initial choices: `_lay2` replaces four mid layers to keep the reference's light, economical sketch; `_dk11` reduces the late shadow gain from 1.3 to 1.1; `_sep4` reduces ink absorption from 8 to 4; `_dwg100` reduces doubled drawing width to 1.0. The small curated tapered paths further control line weight. The five-pigment palette is ochre, raw sienna, burnt sienna, sepia and ultramarine: warm tabby and skin, mixed neutral curls. Minimum shape area 64 native cells retains small eye planes that a whole-sheet default could drop. No changes to physics or painter code.

Before physics: admission and compiled geometry must pass with no unsupported straight seam, no absent path, and no unread chosen option. Inspect initial drawing, pale wash, middle values and pre-closing state as useful. Whole-sheet checkpoint intent: opening is a sparse placing sketch; first wash is deliberately pale; middle layers establish amber cat and charcoal hair while the face stays light; last glazes establish contact and focal darks. Do not judge the first wash as a finished image. The fixed tool is the only allowed painting route, so the first opening/light passage acts as the fast proof; no separate crop physics render is attempted.

Judge in the guide's order: thumbnail value shapes; correctly placed/toned lights; crisp focal and soft/lost nonfocal edges; eye spacing, muzzle length, ears and smile; purposeful connected marks; light-to-dark progress. Failure signs: erased catchlights, large white iris holes, heavy tracing, smeared lids, charcoal hair split into pale islands, fuzzy over-inked contours, stippled fur, uniform scratches, or painted background. Revise only future work if a checkpoint exposes one. Final acceptance also requires complete passage paths, material/recording checks and a finished painting; execution success is separate from visual quality.

## Admission evidence

`bash bench/check.sh` returned ADMITTED. Full-width compilation has 26 pigment passes and 910 connected shape instructions, with zero unsupported axis-aligned seams. It keeps approximately 59.8% paper; 322 shadow instructions cross descriptive region boundaries. The estimated support width is 6.99 cells at the median and 17.27 cells at the 90th percentile (1.36 and 3.37 mm). These are shape-width estimates, not measured brush radii. W13 computes each path's radius as half its local transverse support span, with a one-cell floor. Thus a typical seven-cell narrow plane uses about 3.5-cell radius, whereas a 2–4-cell lid line is only about 0.6–1.1 times that radius and belongs in the fine pen layer. Actual per-stroke radii remain in the executor's recorded geometry. The compiled material log confirms sepia ink and the requested five paint pigments; the fixed base adds a granulating ultramarine slot for three near-blue background cells.
