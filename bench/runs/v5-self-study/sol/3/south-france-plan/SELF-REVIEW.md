# Independent review, V5 try 3

Written after viewing the target, previous-painting.png and stages.jpg, before opening FEEDBACK.md.

The previous painting retains the hillside composition, the main right-hand house positions, and several recognizable upright cypresses. The pale ochre and cool shadow pigments are credible transparent watercolor, and the opening drawing avoids filling dark surfaces with ink.

The dominant failure is value structure. The reference has connected, leafy green canopy masses, a deep central shadow, and crisp warm buildings emerging from that vegetation. The painting substitutes a pale grey-green blanket, many isolated dark islands, and bright punched-out gaps. The foreground tree is almost bare despite being a substantial green crown in the reference. The houses are scattered light fragments rather than coherent lit fronts, violet side planes and roof shapes. The distant architecture is especially weak. Its thin box outlines do not compensate for missing painted planes.

The palette is too grey and desaturated for the green/gold foliage, and the many tiny marks read as camouflage rather than clustered leaves. Paper lights are too numerous and equally bright. The light-wash style is appropriate, but lightness must come from restrained washes surrounding connected shadows, rather than making every major plane weak.

The failure starts before the final accents. At 10% and 25%, the warm wash already breaks into scattered patches. At 50%, a broad diagonal grey-green fill establishes the wrong muted canopy value and weak separation of trees, buildings and terraces. At 75%, separate foliage patches multiply instead of forming shadow groups. The last quarter increases small dark fragments without restoring the large masses. The drawing is sparse in the wrong places: the foreground tree has an angular bare branch diagram, while roof junctions and window recesses lack convincing weight. It should be a small opening scaffold with more purposeful closing structural lines, not a complete dense trace.

My finished painting score: **3.4/10**. My process plausibility score: **2.5/5**. The transparent layering and broad mid-stage wash are plausible, but uniformly scattered flecks accumulate mechanically, with little evidence of working a tree or building as a connected subject.

Proposed direction before reading feedback: preserve the correct composition; give the foliage a warmer, greener connected base and stronger grouped shadows; use fewer larger wash passages; recover coherent architectural planes; reserve only real small highlights; strengthen selected structural drawing while keeping foliage primarily paint; avoid late all-over detail.
