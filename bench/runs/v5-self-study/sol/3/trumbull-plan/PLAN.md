## Resolution and drawing

One native cell = 0.2333 mm. One target pixel = four native cells = 0.9333 mm. Wall and roof masses are tens to hundreds of cells wide and are wash questions. Arch interiors roughly 14–20 target pixels wide span 56–80 native cells; lancets around 2–4 target pixels span 8–16 cells and hold fitted paint. Mullions, arch ribs and flue joints around 0.3–0.7 target pixels span 1.2–2.8 cells: below the approximately four-cell paint-body threshold, therefore selective ink-and-wash. No whole-sheet density change is available or requested.
