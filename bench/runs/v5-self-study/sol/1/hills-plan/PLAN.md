## Style and executable choices

The woodland uses the ink-and-wash profile: connected local-color foundations, a full dry, then a connected shadow glaze. The meadow and pale track families use the precise-watercolor value-plane profile within this loose ink-and-wash composition: pale foundation, two transparent middle layers on dry paint, then restrained terrain shadows. This preserves their luminous yellow-green local color separately from muted woodland crown tops. No atmospheric-wet region is justified by the reference. Overlapping family bounding boxes label whole target-derived color shapes; cellfamilies is off, so their box edges do not become painted divisions. The engine retains fitted irregular geometry. I do not request stroke lanes or additional texture passes.

The six pans are yellow ochre, hansa yellow, French ultramarine, cerulean, sepia and sap green. Yellow plus green carries sunlit grass; blue plus green and sepia carries woodland; dilute ochre carries the track. Black ink is explicitly declared as black-ink, with opening pressure capped at .25 and closing pressure capped at .50. It must appear as black ink in the material log. This overrides the base sepia material rather than tinting the final export.

Relative to V096, darkgain changes from 1.3 to 1.1 to retain separations within the wooded masses; coolgain changes from 1.3 to 1.1 to keep olive crowns from turning uniformly blue; drawing width gain changes from 2 to 1 so the sparse branches remain subordinate to the washes. The physical nib profile remains responsible for final contact width. lay2 replaces four middle layers with two for the meadow/track value planes; woodland has no middle layers. skysplit is explicitly zero so first washes separate the two technique/style domains instead of averaging grass with crown tops. drawinglastmin is .2, admitting the reviewed light branch paths while their individual pressure remains low. All other useful base behavior remains in force.

Revision 2 adds cur: use neutral white paper (252/251/248), keep actual target-white detection, and remove the relative-paper subtraction that mistakes the full-bleed crop's cream road for paper. The existing physical sheet already has this RGB; this changes future value planning and reserve classification, not the exported color of earlier washes. The road should be eligible for pale warm paint in the remaining ordinary passages/touch-ups.

## Region techniques and order

Every region chooses from the watercolor technique reference. Required paper state means measured state over the entire stroke footprint and its neighbors, checked by the fixed executor before every stroke. Wait or dry when a dry placement is not ready; do not infer readiness from elapsed time. Within one connected wash, its own wet overlap is allowed; separate pigments and the later glaze follow the executor's full drying checkpoints. The physical schedule selects some soft tree-interior passages as wet on wet: allow deliberate clean-water preparation and a medium pigment charge there, checking measured moist/sheened paper before color. This is a named local break for the reference's merging blue-green crown interiors; preserve dry barriers at trunk forks and meadow/path boundaries. It does not authorize a wet whole-sheet blur.

## Lights and glazes

The cream road is a pale painted plane, not a broad rectangular bare-paper reserve. Near-paper flecks already detected from the target are bare-paper lights. Bright meadow and crown tops are pale painted planes protected by the value ladder. Small foliage gaps are negative-painted target openings. No artificial reserve rectangles are added: their straight sides would damage the curved track and irregular crowns. No lift is required. If a held light looks excessively white, inspect its local pale wash before considering a revision; do not attempt opaque light recovery.

The main shadow glaze crosses the central belt's blue-green and olive crown shapes, trunks and adjoining tree groups; the near-left glaze crosses the focal tree into its neighboring shadowed trees; the near-right glaze crosses its connected woodland group. Each begins on fully dry underpaint and uses the executor's connected fitted shadow support. The meadow's terrain shadow may cross adjacent green/yellow local-color shapes but must leave the warm path luminous. No dark-first departure is requested. Where the physical tree-interior schedule chooses a soft charge, deliberately prepare that local dry base with clean water and check its measured state before the color charge, as described above.

## Checkpoints and acceptance

Before GPU: admission must pass; inspect the compiled value plan for target-derived irregular connected shapes, actual dry-state requirements, cross-region shadows, nonzero path support, and material declaration. The eight opening lines should establish tree scale and the central fork without tracing the entire composition.

After early pigment passages: the sheet should read as luminous pale/local-color washes with the track light. Incomplete color mixtures are judged as incomplete foundations, not as final color. After the full foundation: green slopes should connect and cool woodland should have lighter crown planes. Before closing: the path must read continuously through the dark woods; shadows should have depth and remain mixed, and the foreground meadow should remain bright. Closing ink must add a few recognizable forks, never a fill or a universal contour.

Accept only against the skill's final judging list: four value masses and focal bend at thumbnail size; correctly located and toned lights; chosen firm, soft and lost edges; the path's bend/width and tree landmarks; purposeful tapered marks without stipple, equal stamps, scratches or tiled patches; visibly light-to-dark progress; untouched light paper appropriate to this full-bleed landscape, with detail concentrated near the bend. Failure signals are a white road rail, neon field or blue-only canopy, uniform blur, ink-heavy texture, disconnected pale tree islands, hard rectangular seams, missing focal forks, or a dark noisy foreground. Revise only unpainted passages after locating the earliest visible failing layer. Record each revision and its reason here.
