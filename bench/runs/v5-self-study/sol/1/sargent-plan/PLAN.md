## Material and executable choices

Six pans: yellow ochre, burnt sienna, French ultramarine, cerulean blue, sepia watercolor and sap green. Blue plus sienna/sepia supplies the neutral shadows; weak sap green with blue and ochre supplies the right water. Sepia is a watercolor pan here. Declare warm cream paper RGB 230/224/208, just above the source's brightest 1% mean 227/220/203; the automatic fallback 252/251/248 would make the white hull unnaturally brilliant. This is physical sheet color, chosen before painting. Both drawing files have no paths and medium none; `--drawing none` disables opening and closing drawing. No graphite, pen, gouache, splatter or post-export color changes.

Changes from V096: two middle layers rather than four (`lay2`) to keep broad light planes and avoid nested contour bands; cool gain 1.0 rather than 1.3 (`cg10`) so the bow stays grey-blue; dark gain 1.2 rather than 1.3 (`dk12`) to retain strong accents without a black blanket; disable sky lanes (`noslane`) because a curved bow must not become horizontal sky stripes; move 30% of qualifying small-feature mid demand into final dry accents (`acc3`). This avoids dividing the pale foundation into hundreds of individually dried color fragments. Disable the extra sky tube and the generic lower-sheet ground glaze: there is no sky or pavement. Ordinary connected shadow glazes remain, using the fitted local mixture.

All regions start wet on dry deliberately: this crop shows shaped strokes, sharp rigging and white hulls, rather than an atmospheric open sky. A flowing loaded brush can merge its own same-passage contacts while preserving the adjacent dry light shape. This is a chosen alternative to wet-in-wet, supported by the reference's boat edges. Water .62 and .45 refer to the executor's brush-load values, not invented paper readings. Pigment strength is calibrated per fitted passage by the fixed engine.

Before every stroke the executor must measure the actual footprint and neighboring paper. New mid shapes and glazes require fully dry paper; drying must establish the measured state. Same-passage wet overlap is permitted for connected washes. Never lay a fresh watery accent into damp paint; wait/dry. No gate is disabled. Clean-water softening only follows the planner's selected soft boundaries; no global blur.
