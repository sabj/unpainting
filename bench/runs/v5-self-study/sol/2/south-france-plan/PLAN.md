## Paper state, drawing and glaze continuity

Before every stroke, the fixed executor checks measured paper state over the entire footprint and neighboring halo. Wet-on-dry starts only on dry paper. Same-passage overlaps may merge intentionally; other wet neighbors require drying, waiting or working elsewhere. Each shade and drawing layer needs its full dry gate. Never invent a reading from a timer. Genuine pigment/water transport, settling, granulation and normal Fast drying remain active.

Connected shadow glazes cross target color/family boundaries: house wall to eave/garden/earth; neighboring olive interiors to cypresses and ground; foreground canopy to root/earth shade. With all families urban, no separate style domain divides these. Lights are kept out or receive only their pale foundation. The fixed executor chooses real damp-brush softening only on supported soft boundaries; no optical blur.
