Replace precise-watercolor's two middle layers and two shadow layers with the ink-and-wash foundation plus one shadow glaze. `region_w13_lay0_hue_dk11_cg10_sep3_dwg139` removes middle layers, removes the light-stage hue-domain split while retaining fitted pigment demands, reduces shadow gain from 1.3 to 1.1, and retains neutral cool gain and the finer sepia recipe. They are bare paper throughout. The fixed planner also simplifies connected near-paper target lights: bib, muzzle, forehead, nose ridge, ear interiors and background. Target-supported thin white whiskers through orange are negative spaces; do not replace them with opaque white or extra black strokes. No masked broad box, lifting or white overlay is requested.

Foundation passages connect local planes using original fitted paths and allow neighbours of the same passage to merge while open. Detail stays dry next to wet marks; do not let a large wash spill into an eye. All later shadow passages start on fully dry paint. Shadow support ignores descriptive family boundaries: cat cheek into neck/flank, cat/portrait contact, and portrait socket into cheek/lower face. The compiler must show cross-region support. There is no dark-first exception. Foundation-before-shadow is the deliberately shorter version of the light-to-dark model, justified by the high-key sketch rather than a need to cover the whole photograph with five washes.

## Executor and checkpoint acceptance

Before each brush or pen stroke, require measured paper state across the footprint and its neighbours. Dry-state passages wait, dry or work elsewhere if the base is not ready; same-passage overlap is allowed. Never infer dryness from the clock. Watery foundation and medium glaze are distinct from pigment concentration; use the fixed calibrated finite instrument loads. No physics effect is disabled, no export recolour is used, and no painter code is changed.

Run `bench/check.sh`; require ADMITTED, no unsupported straight edges, readable fitted value geometry and connected glazes. The allowed fast proof is this painting's opening and first-wash checkpoints; do not start a separate physics run.

At opening, both faces must already be recognizable as sparse lines, with no filled hair or heavy lower lids. After the first warm pigment, expect some cat and skin colour while the hair remains chiefly drawing: this incomplete state is intentional. After all foundation pigments, expect pale local colour, an under-value hair mass and intact large lights. The shadow stage should visibly select smaller dark areas rather than darken every plane in lockstep. Before closing, paint must carry the large value masses; final drawing adds only supported focal and directional lines. Stop at the earliest visible failure and change only passages still to come.

Accept changes only against the technique guide's final judging list: thumbnail value shapes; lights in position and naturally toned; hard/soft/lost edges in the reference's roles; eye, nose, mouth and ear landmarks; connected purposeful marks with tapered ends, without tiles/stipple/hatching; plausible light-to-dark progression; untouched paper and focal detail concentration. Material execution, admission and artistic quality are separate verdicts. Reject swollen irises, lost glints, heavy under-eye creases, global blur, repeated angular islands, ink-filled hair, abrupt texture dumps or seams. A successful render alone is not artistic acceptance.
