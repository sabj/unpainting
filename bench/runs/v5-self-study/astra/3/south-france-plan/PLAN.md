## Executable order and moisture

1. Opening selective black pen on measured dry paper. It should already identify the stepped houses and pine, with much blank sheet.
2. Warm, green and cool local-colour wash passes, water .5 from urban-sketch, weak pigment scaled .8. Medium flowing contact; connected own-passage overlap may remain open. Bright planes get their local colour at this stage; dark masses remain lighter for the later glaze.
3. Dry and remeasure. Shadow pass allocation over the coherent .45-threshold support, same style water .5, medium pigment, dark gain 1. These glazes cross descriptive families: canopy into neighbouring canopy and shaded ground; wall side through the eave and adjoining recess. Each pigment passage dries before another. No blanket middle value ladder or corrective texture rounds.
4. Dry, inspect before closing, then selective black closing pen. No global last-minute increase unless the actual stopped sheet warrants it.

The fixed executor must check measured paper over each footprint and neighbours before every mark, waiting/drying/rechecking if needed. Only verified own-passage overlap may be wet. No timer substitutes for paper measurements. No wet-in-wet detail exception, disabled physics or external bitmap painting.
