# Trumbull — precise architectural watercolor

## Reference cues
- Warm sunlit limestone (ochre / raw sienna) on the main gable and tower faces; cooler blue-grey shadow on the left return wall. Connected cool slate roofs with chimney stacks.
- Pale graded sky with soft white cloud corridors — must stay open, no hard banding.
- Foreground: small green trees, street furniture, traffic lights, pedestrians — subordinate, not competing with stone mass.
- Dark window recesses (near-black) and arched openings — painted last on dried wall, never averaged into wall washes.

## Regions & technique
- **sun-main-gable** (centre, 32–72% x): wet on dry, broad warm ochre planes; stone-block hints (blk60/bmm40) give block variation.
- **cool-main-return** (left, 2–34% x): wet on dry, cooler darker value for shadowed return wall.
- **slate-roofs** (top band): wet on dry, connected cool slate with restrained tile variation.
- **sky** (top 0–22% y): wet on dry graded wash; sky lanes + skyblue give soft horizontal gradation.
- **foliage-street** (bottom): wet on dry, sap green / olive for trees, subordinate street detail.
- **whole-physical-fringe**: descriptive fringe for on-sheet overhang; no geometry expansion.

All families use precise-watercolor style: 5 washes, per-layer calibrated water, crisp focal detail on arches/windows, softened turning edges, lost non-focal edges.

## Moisture & load
- Per-layer calibrated load (waterLevel) — pale first washes, building to mid-values, two final shadow glazes.
- Wet-mix 0.3, bridge 0.82 — controlled soft transitions at shadow edges, dry otherwise for crisp stone joints.
- Cold-press paper, physical 1 — granulation in sky, texture in stone.

## Protected lights
- No explicit reserves — painter's own near-paper target lights preserve brightest stone trim, window mullions, sky whites and sidewalk. Explicit reserves would create unsupported axis-aligned seams on this facade.

## Edges & values
- Crisp focal detail on arched windows, gable coping, chimney; one softened turning edge (gable return); lost edges in foliage/street.
- Light-to-dark ladder: pale stone base → mid ochre → slate blue-grey → shadow glaze (ultramarine + warm earth, violet lift) → recess darks.
- Darkgain 1.3, coolgain 1.3 — matches Trumbull's deep window recesses and cool shadow.

## Drawing
- Opening: traced sepia ink (trace-ink .12/14) — structural outlines, window bays, roof lines. Closing: denser traced ink (.10/10) for selective focal reinforcement on arches and signage. Base recipe sep8/ink4.

## Tokens
- Base recipe as-is (region_w13_lay4_ink4_rec2x_rcl_sky3_leaf_sep8_cg13_vio_rg10_blk60_bmm40_bka20_birr_skyb_cpp_dk13_tv8_uncap_pgreen_dwg200_tp2_shs) — no overrides. Trumbull palette (pgreen) via base, stone blocks, sky lanes, violet shadow, ground glaze all active.

## Failure signs
- Muddy stone (over-mixed washes), lost sky (banding or over-painted whites), window recesses averaged into wall (no dark punch), foliage competing with architecture, hard rectangular seams from reserves/regions.
