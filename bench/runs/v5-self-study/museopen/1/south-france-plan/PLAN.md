# Plan — France (south-france) — urban sketch, pen plus light wash

## Reference cues
- Provençal hill village: clustered stone houses with terracotta roofs on wooded hillside.
- Foreground dominant tree (lower-left), dark cypress spires behind, warm ochre walls, red-orange roofs.
- Light is warm, high-key; much white paper should remain. Pen carries structure.

## Regions & technique (order = light to dark, as painter does)
1. **sky / distant hills** [0,0,1,0.32] — wet on dry, very pale flat wash (cerulean/ultramarine + ochre). Keep paper white dominant. No hard seam: wash grades into village.
2. **village / architecture** [0.12,0.08,0.92,0.68] — pen defines roof edges, windows, walls first (opening ink). Then light flat washes: ochre/sienna for walls, burnt sienna for roofs. Second mid layer for shadow sides.
3. **foliage** [0,0.35,1,1] — wet on dry, sap green + ochre + ultramarine mixes. Foreground tree darkest; midground trees lighter. Pen for trunk/branches.

## Moisture & load
- urban-sketch style: one light flat wash (washCount 1), water 0.5 throughout. Light load, dry quickly.
- lay2 (2 mid layers) keeps washes pale; noslane avoids sky banding.

## Protected lights (reserves)
- No explicit reserves; painter's auto paper lights preserve brightest walls and sky. Previous small reserves left white squares (value-plan artefacts) so removed.

## Edges & values
- Edges: confident connected pen junctions; washes stay inside pen. No wet-in-wet soft edges needed.
- Values: white paper = lightest; pale washes mid; pen + deepest foliage/roof shadows darkest. Darkgain 1.3 from base.

## What would show failure
- Muddy washes covering white paper; pen buried under paint; sky too dark; foliage as flat green without pen; unsupported straight reserve edges.

## V5 changes
- First try: urban-sketch with lay2/ink4/sep4/noslane, traced pen lines for opening+closing, 3 families. Removed reserves that caused white-square artefacts; kept opening/closing pressure at 1 for strong pen.
