# Mittens - Watercolor Portrait Sketch Plan

## Reference Cues
- Left: orange/white cat (Mittens) with green eyes, white chest, orange tabby markings
- Right: person holding cat, warm skin tones, dark hair, gentle expression
- Background: clean white paper, vignette style portrait sketch
- Lighting: soft diffused, light washes, fine ink lines define form

## Regions & Technique
- No large families: painter's own color/value segmentation handles cat vs person naturally
- Reserves: tiny eye catchlights (2-3mm) to preserve sparkle in cat and person eyes
- Overall style: precise-watercolor - light-to-dark progression, reserved lights, dry focal edges

## Moisture & Load
- Light washes: lay3 (3 mid layers) instead of 4, dk11 (darkgain 1.1) for lighter values
- Base pgreen, sky tokens are Trumbull/architecture only and will be skipped
- Per-layer calibrated load, wet-on-dry for crisp details, glazed for skin

## Protected Lights
- Paper whites: background, cat white chest/fur, eye catchlights via reserves
- Painter also keeps near-paper lights automatically

## Edges & Values
- Soft lost edges in background, crisp focal edges on eyes/nose
- Light value ladder: preserve paper for highlights, build form with transparent glazes
- Fine sepia ink lines (ink4) for whiskers, fur direction, facial features

## Failure Modes
- Over-dark washes would lose light sketch quality
- Muddy skin tones from too many pigments
- Lost eye catchlights
- Hard rectangular seams (avoided by no large families/reserves)

## Changes
- Initial try: lay3_dk11 for lighter washes, small reserves only, no families to avoid unsupported seams
