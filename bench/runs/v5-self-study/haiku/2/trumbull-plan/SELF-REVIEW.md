# Self-Review: Try 1

## What Worked
- **Overall composition**: Building placement and proportions are correct
- **Window placement**: The five main window groups are in the right positions
- **Color family**: Warm wall tone and blue windows are in the right range
- **Foliage placement**: Foliage on right side is present and roughly textured
- **Value range**: The finished painting does go from light to dark across the 100% progression

## What Went Wrong

### Early Stages Too Faint (Most Critical)
- At 10% and 25% strokes, the painting was nearly invisible - judges noted "whole sheet at one faint value, as if faded in"
- Initial washes were too pale/weak to read
- This made the progression feel artificial rather than like a person painting
- **Fix**: Use richer, more saturated initial washes so early stages are visible and show clear value progression

### Stone Texture Artificial and Scattered
- The wall shows random scattered spots and patches rather than organized stone coursing
- The target clearly shows horizontal layers of stone blocks
- Current texture looks like "texture arriving as a dump" - appearing suddenly rather than building gradually
- **Fix**: Use ordered dry-brush strokes following actual horizontal coursing lines in the target
- **Fix**: Paint stone texture as part of mid-value layers following architectural structure, not as random scattered accents

### Edges Not Sharp Enough
- Edge detail score: 2.67/5 (low)
- Windows and mullions blur together instead of reading clearly
- Window recesses lack crisp definition
- **Fix**: Paint windows on completely dry paper with controlled load for crisp edges
- **Fix**: Use closing ink more selectively to define mullions where they clarify form

### Wall Tone Issues
- The wall reads as too greenish-olive rather than warm golden-tan
- Less luminosity than the target
- **Fix**: Use warmer pigments (yellower ochres, warmer siennas) in base wash
- **Fix**: Ensure lit wall plane stays light and warm, shadow side is darker but still warm (not greenish)

### Value Separation Weak
- Lit and shadow sides of building not distinct enough
- Windows and cast shadows don't pop forward
- **Fix**: Paint shadow glaze more deliberately, ensuring it's clearly darker than lit wall
- **Fix**: Leave more white paper for lit highlights and sharp edges

### Progression Judge Concerns
- "Feature painted before its form" - some marks appear in wrong sequence
- "Diagonal light-ray streaks before form established" - artifacts in early stages
- "Everything sharpening at once late" - detail arrives suddenly around 75% instead of gradually
- **Fix**: More careful ordering of passes
- **Fix**: Build detail progressively, especially around architectural focal points

## Disagreement with Judges
- I accept the low vision scores (3.0) - there were real issues with early-stage visibility
- The classic edge score (9.43) is surprisingly high - they rated edges better than my own assessment suggests
- Vision overall (3.0) is fair given the early faintness and sudden texture arrival

## My Score: 4/10 (finished), 2/5 (progression)
- Composition is there but execution has clear problems
- Early stages don't read as human painting; texture appears artificial
- Needs richer washes, organized stone detail, sharper edges, better value separation

## Changes for Try 2

1. **Richer initial washes**: Use more pigment in early sky and wall washes so the painting reads at 10% and 25% strokes
2. **Explicit regions**: Define sky, lit wall, shadow wall, windows, foliage in config.json so painter understands structure
3. **Stone coursing structure**: Opening lines should include horizontal stone-layer guidance; dry-brush should follow this pattern
4. **Window reserves**: Clearly reserve window shapes and arches so they stay protected and sharp
5. **Token adjustment**: Test `_lay4` to build values more gradually, or keep `_lay3` with richer pigment concentration
6. **Warmer pigments**: Emphasize golden yellows and warm siennas over cooler olive greens
7. **Better shadow glaze**: One clear glaze across shadow side, not scattered touches
