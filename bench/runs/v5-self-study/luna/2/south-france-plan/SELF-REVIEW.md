# Try 1 self-review

## Independent read, before the external feedback

**Finished painting: 4/10. Painting plausibility: 2/5.**

The composition still carries the target's broad layout: the clustered hillside roofs sit in the right half, and the wooded slope fills the foreground and left. The warm roof and wall colors are appealing, and some pale breaks suggest sun on the village.

The strongest problem is the foliage. It resolves into many small, hard-edged olive and yellow fragments rather than connected tree masses with a few intentional leaf marks. The tall near-black shapes through the center and left are too stark and read as holes or cutouts, not cypress shadows. Large paper-white gaps interrupt the hillside and buildings. The architecture is recognizable by position and roof color, but its walls, roof planes and openings break into patches, so the village feels less clear than in the reference. There is little visible pen character to support the requested urban sketch, pen plus light wash style; the image reads mostly as a dense, angular paint collage.

The stage strip suggests the broad layout and pale start were already present at 10% of strokes. The first major loss of clarity appears as the mid-value foliage fragments build by 25%: the trees stop reading as broad connected masses and the white gaps become conspicuous. The dark accents visible by 50% deepen that problem, particularly the vertical black cypress-like strokes. Later stages mostly reinforce the same fragmented texture instead of restoring a clear value hierarchy. I would keep the warm village colors and overall placement, but simplify and connect the canopy washes, soften the distant tree masses, reserve fewer and smaller lights, and keep the darkest accents sparse. For this urban-sketch brief, a few deliberate structural pen lines should clarify roofs and openings without outlining every paint shape.

## After reading the external feedback

I agree with the process panel's low early-stage scores and its notes about the faint, slab-like opening and texture arriving as a dump. The checkpoints show the count rising from 1,038 strokes before the closing boundary to 8,700 at it, which supports the concern that the final drawing pass contributes too much at once. The finished-painting panel's low marks-and-texture and edges-and-detail criteria also match my view of the fragmented foliage and weakly stated architecture.

I disagree with the process judge's description of the finished frame as a convincing sketch. The placement is recognizable, but the paint remains patchy and the closing marks do not read as selective, purposeful pen structure to me. I also give less weight to the classic edge score of 9.11: it conflicts with the visible fragmentation and the low visual-panel edges-and-detail result, and these judges are noisy by the task brief. I will keep the warm palette and recognizable layout rather than treating the composite 4.78 as evidence that the dark foliage and broken lights are successful.
