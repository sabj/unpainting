## Recipe changes and checkpoints

This is the first learning attempt, so there is no prior critique. Relative to the V096 base, `_lay2` uses two middle washes to keep the portrait economical, `_dk11` reduces the inherited dark gain so the light paper planes survive, `_ol18` caps the hand-selected opening scaffold at 18 gestures, and `_ink3` lowers closing-line share for fine sepia accents. The fixed cold-press paper and the existing physical drying and recording route stay in use. The palette is yellow ochre, raw sienna, burnt sienna, ultramarine, sepia, and quinacridone rose: warm earths for the tabby and skin, ultramarine/sepia for the mixed hair shadow, and rose for the small warm face notes.

At the opening stop, check that the cat's two eyes, nose, and whisker fan and the person's two eyes, nose, and mouth are placed before pigment. After the first wash, the cat and face should read as pale masses with a large paper share. After the middle passes, landmarks should be crisp and the ginger patches should remain separate from the white blaze. After the shadow passes, the hair should lead the darks while the white muzzle and facial lights remain. Before closing ink, verify that the paint is dry. Visible failure is any halo around a box, a dark first wash, lost eye glints, smeared facial landmarks, disconnected specks, or uniform line weight; change only the unpainted stage if one appears.
