# Self-review of try 1 (written before opening FEEDBACK.md)

I looked at `bench/feedback/previous-painting.png`, the target and `stages.jpg`. I also saw in
`previous-checkpoints.jsonl` that I painted try 1 with zero looks and zero revisions: I started it and left. So
nothing in that painting was corrected by looking.

## What works
- Layout and likeness at thumbnail size are right: cat head upper left, man's head lower right, the hair as the one
  big dark mass, the cat's brown patches pressing against the man's cheek, most of the sheet bare paper.
- The sheet reads as a light-wash sketch: lots of untouched paper, no flood of ink, nothing filled solid.
- The man's eyes, brows and the cat's dark upper lid are in the right places and are among the crispest, darkest marks.
- The hair has a mottled, ragged-edged body with paper flecks, not a flat grey fill.

## What is wrong
1. **Colour is the biggest miss.** The target has strong orange on the cat, blue-black hair, and green-grey cat eyes.
   My painting is one sepia/tan family: the cat is muddy tan, the hair is warm brown-grey, the cat's eyes lost their
   green. It looks like a sepia photo filter, not watercolour. This was there from the 10% stage on (the first pale
   washes were already beige), so it is a palette decision (I left the pans to the painter) and not a late accident.
2. **Square white catchlights.** The four rectangular reserves show as hard white squares, like dead pixels. They read
   as stamped, not as glints. (I used four small rectangular reserves. Wrong tool for the job.)
3. **Long thin ink lines that do not hug an edge.** A hairline curves across the cat's body from about (465,485) to
   (620,860) and another runs near-vertical down the cat/man boundary at x~610. They look ruled and float over the
   washes. They came from my hand-placed lines (opening and closing), placed with too few points and no pressure
   variation.
4. **Whiskers and fine line work are mostly missing.** The target's whiskers and soft pencil contours are a large part
   of "fine lines". I drew about seven whiskers and they barely show. The ask was "fine lines and light washes" and I
   delivered light washes only.
5. **Terraced contour islands.** Every wash is a stack of posterised value islands with hard rims (the cat's face and
   the man's left cheek show onion-ring bands). Real watercolour has fewer, larger shapes and one soft edge per shape.
6. **The man's face is a mask.** Forehead and cheeks are one flat bare-paper shape with a hard scalloped edge against
   the hair, and a banded halo on the left cheek. The target's skin has a soft warm shadow along the left and under the
   brow and a lot of tonal modelling around the nose and mouth. The mouth and nose are almost absent.
7. **Cat face lost its structure.** The target's cat has an orange mask running between the eyes and down to the nose,
   with white muzzle and whisker pads. Mine is a scatter of brown blobs; the nose and mouth are small dark smudges.
8. **Lower cat body is a faint ghost.** The vertical orange strips at lower left are pale and shapeless.

## At which stage it went wrong
- Palette: at the very first wash (10%).
- Reserve squares: visible from the first wash and never softened.
- Banding and posterisation: grew through the mid layers (25% to 50%). From 50% to 100% almost nothing changed except
  the hair getting darker. The last half of the painting adds little.
- Lines: the closing pass added thin straight-ish lines that did not match the wash edges.
- Process: I never looked, so I never corrected anything.

## My scores for try 1
- Finished painting: **4 / 10.** Right layout and tonal distribution, wrong colour, stamped catchlights, ruled lines,
  few whiskers, banded edges.
- Plausibly painted: **3 / 5.** The sequence (pencil, pale washes, darker patches, hair, then small darks) is a real
  order and bare paper stays. But the strips show posterised islands rather than brush strokes, and the later stages
  barely differ.
