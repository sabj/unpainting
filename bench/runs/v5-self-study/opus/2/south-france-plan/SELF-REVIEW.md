# Self-review of try 1 (SF, round V5)

Written from `previous-painting.png`, `compare.png`, `stages.jpg`, the previous plan files and checkpoints, before
opening `FEEDBACK.md`.

## What works

- The big placements are roughly right: the cypress verticals upper left of centre, the warm block of the tower houses on
  the right, the foreground pine's silhouette at lower left (it has a trunk and a conical crown), the blue-green
  distant hills at top right.
- The colour family of the greens (olive, yellow-green, grey-green) and the terracotta roofs is in the target's range.
- The stage order is light to dark in principle: pale yellow first, then mid greens, then darks.

## What is wrong

1. **A pink sheet.** The painter's own palette pick included quinacridone rose, and the light ground band went down as
   three full-sheet ground washes (hansa, then rose, then cerulean). The rose shows wherever later paint does not cover:
   the whole margin, a diagonal stripe through the centre, the gaps in the canopy. There is no pink in the target. This is
   the single worst defect and it was decided at plan time (no `--paints`).
2. **Camouflage patchwork instead of trees.** The olive slope, left slope and lower right are hundreds of hard-edged,
   equally sized blotches in three or four greens. No canopy reads as one mass with a lit side and a shadow side. The mark
   character is uniform noise. Four mid layers (`_lay4`) each fragmenting the painter's segmentation is the cause.
3. **No urban sketch.** The requested style is pen plus light wash. The sheet is nearly fully covered (the plan alone
   covered 99.6%), the paint is dense and opaque-looking, and the pen is barely visible: the opening lines were 0.1 mm
   hairlines and the closing pen is lost in the busy paint. A sketcher would leave much more paper and let the line carry
   the buildings.
4. **The lights are in the wrong places.** The lightest plane of the target (the sunlit ochre terraces upper left and the
   lit tower walls) is a scatter of white flecks and ochre chips; random white islands appear in the middle of the
   canopy where the target has mid greens.
5. **Focal point lost.** The tower houses have no cube form: roofs are flat orange triangles, walls white rectangles, the
   violet shadow sides and dark eaves are missing. Nothing on the sheet says "village".
6. **Foreign darks.** Navy-black blots in the centre (x 0.35 to 0.6, y 0.35 to 0.7) do not exist in the target; the
   darkest target values there are deep greens in canopy hollows, not blue-black.
7. **Mechanical seams.** A diagonal light stripe through the centre, a long straight diagonal edge at right, and a
   ragged rectangular border; at 25% the horizontal lane tiles of the ground wash are visible.

## Where it went wrong

- At planning: no palette override (rose entered), urban sketch requested but the full value ladder and four mid layers
  kept, no light reserves for the terraces and walls, faint opening pen.
- At the first wash (10 to 25%): full-sheet hansa and rose grounds with the dry-brush stripe; the later revisions
  (`--drytooth`, `_spl6` then back) chased the stripe instead of the palette and the coverage.
- At the mid layers (50%): the canopy fragmented into patchwork and never recovered; 50% to 100% added only dark blots.

## My scores

- Finished painting: **2 / 10**. Placement is recognisable at thumbnail, but the pink, the patchwork and the missing pen
  make it neither an urban sketch nor a watercolour of the village.
- Process plausibility: **2 / 5**. The order (pale, mid, dark) is human, but a full-sheet pink ground with lane tiles and a
  straight stripe, then camouflage blotches laid everywhere at once, is not how a person paints this.

## Against the judges' critique

Judges: both vision judges 2/10 (composition/values 3 to 4, edges 2, marks 1 to 2); classic value 5.7, edge 9.26, marks
6.84 (the third judge never ran: spend cap). Progress judge: 2, 2, 2, 3, 4 at 10/25/50/75/100%.

Where I agree:

- **2/10 overall** matches my 2/10. Low edges and marks scores fit the camouflage patchwork and the missing pen.
- **"Flat diagonal colour bands bounded by straight travelling edges" and "a straight travelling front, a wipe"** at
  10 to 25%: yes. These are the full-sheet ground passes (one per pigment) laid in lanes, with the stripe. A person would
  draw first and then wash local colour area by area.
- **"Texture arriving as a dump" at 50%**: yes. 25% was all ground; by 50% the whole canopy patchwork arrived at once.
- **"Flat evenly-toned slabs standing in for a form"**: yes, the roofs and walls of the towers.
- **The judge note's "pen-then-wash build-up" expectation**: yes, it matches the style (urban sketch) and the style
  profile's own order (Cray/Haseler: pen first). The opening pen was too faint to be seen as a first stage.

Where I disagree:

- **75% = 3 and 100% = 4 out of 5** are too generous. The last frames carry the same patchwork and foreign navy blots;
  I would give 2 there too. Noise of half a point is expected, but I do not read the late frames as evidence that the
  late process worked; they look human only because the patchwork resembles busy brushwork at that size.
- **Classic edge 9.26** says nothing useful: the edges are hard everywhere, including where the target is soft.
  I ignore the classic numbers for decisions (pixel-style metrics reward the wrong things, skill section 9).
- **"A region arriving finished while its neighbour is untouched"**: I saw little of this in the strip; the ground
  passes covered everything at once. Not a priority.
- The judges did not name the pink sheet, which I think is the largest single defect; I fix it regardless.
