# Painting Plan — Ships (Round V5, Try 2 of 3)

## 1. Reference Cues & Visual Structure (`bench/plan/target.png`)
The target (`300x244` pixels, painted at `1200x976` on a `280 mm` cold-press watercolor sheet) is a detail of John Singer Sargent's *White Ships* (1908):
- **Warm Cream Paper Ground (`--paperrgb 221,215,199`)**: The sunlit hull on the right (`x: 0.58–1.00, y: 0.00–0.67`), the foreground anchor arm (`x: 0.09–0.30, y: 0.71–0.92`), and the brightest water glints rely on the warm cream paper (`221, 215, 199`) left bare through natural target-light reservation (`--paperlights 1 --paperrel 1`, `9.3%` of the sheet).
- **Sweeping Curved Bow in Cool Shadow (`sargent.shadowed-bow`, `x: 0.18–0.64, y: 0.00–0.78`)**: A broad curving plane of cerulean and French ultramarine blue-grey washes following the flare of the hull.
- **Hawsepipe and Warm Sienna Stem/Chain (`sargent.hawsepipe-rust`, `x: 0.35–0.65, y: 0.00–0.58`)**: A dark oval hawsehole (`x: 0.43–0.53, y: 0.19–0.36`) with a warm burnt-sienna/ochre rust streak along the stem edge and a trail of warm orange-sienna anchor-chain links curving down-left toward the foreground anchor.
- **Left Masts and Foreground Anchor (`sargent.left-rigging-and-anchor`, `x: 0.00–0.34, y: 0.00–0.94`)**: Warm ochre/sienna rigging and masts on the upper left above the cool blue-grey lower hull shadow that frames the sunlit cream-ochre anchor fluke.
- **Hanging Fenders and Diagonal Spar (`sargent.fenders-and-spar`, `x: 0.76–1.00, y: 0.12–0.98`)**: Two compact dark brown-black round fenders hanging near the upper right (`x: 0.78–0.99, y: 0.13–0.27`) beside a diagonal golden-ochre and sienna spar (`x: 0.79–0.96, y: 0.23–0.98`).
- **Waterline Shadow and Turquoise Ripples (`sargent.waterline`, `x: 0.18–1.00, y: 0.66–1.00`)**: Deep ultramarine/sienna shadow at the base of the bow flare (`x: 0.25–0.62, y: 0.76–0.98`) meeting horizontal cerulean and sap-green ripples on the lower right (`x: 0.64–1.00, y: 0.66–0.98`).

## 2. What Changed from Try 1 and Why (V5 Learning Round)

### Changes Made (`region_w15_lay2_hue_noslane_rg0_cg12_dp85_dg2_ms1500_tp1`)
1. **Removed the Artificial Rectangular Reserve (`reserves: []` instead of `[0.21, 0.78, 0.25, 0.82]`)**:
   - *Why*: In Try 1, the rectangular reserve punched a sharp, unnatural axis-aligned white square right out of the foreground anchor fluke and adjacent dark blue shadow from the first wash onward. Because `--paperlights 1 --paperrel 1` natively reserves connected near-paper target highlights along their true organic contours (`108,891` cells, `9.3%` of the sheet), removing the rectangular reserve eliminates the synthetic square hole while preserving all natural highlights.
2. **Explicit 5-Pigment Sargent Marine Palette (`--paints ochre,sienna,ultramarine,cerulean,sapgreen`) + Kubelka-Munk Shape Refit (`--shaperefit 3`)**:
   - *Why*: In Try 1, leaving `--palettewide 1` unconstrained caused it to select `quinacridone rose` and `hansa yellow` instead of earth/marine pigments, laying a pinkish wash across the entire sheet in Stage 1 (`10%` of strokes) and failing to capture the warm burnt-sienna/ochre rust and timbers. Declaring `ochre,sienna,ultramarine,cerulean,sapgreen` (plus the granulating sky ultramarine tube) with `--shaperefit 3` eliminates the pink cast, achieves a `3.63 dE00` palette fit across all 9 local color clusters, and reduces per-shape Kubelka-Munk density error from `0.058` to `0.007`.
3. **Switched from `region_w13` + `_lay4` + `_sky3` + Default `skyLanes` to `region_w15_lay2_hue_noslane`**:
   - *Why*: Both the finished-painting judges (`marks texture: 2`, `edges detail: 3`) and the progression judge (`1/5`, citing *"the whole sheet at one faint value, as if faded in"* and *"texture arriving as a dump, everything sharpening at once late"*) reacted to three mechanical bottlenecks in Try 1:
     - First, `--skylanes 1` (active by default without `_noslane`) treated the cool blue ship bow as a sky and raster-scanned it with ~100 horizontal lines per pigment pass in Band 1 and Band 2 `L0..L3`, spending the first 60% of strokes repeatedly tinting the blue hull. Disabling `skyLanes` (`_noslane`) and enabling `region_w15` (`--brushchoice 1`, `choosePassageBrush`) paints large washes with a `38 mm` mop and `25.4 mm` flat along the plane's natural major axis, while assigning `round12`, `round8`, `round4`, and `rigger` brushes to narrower midtone, shadow, and accent shapes.
     - Second, replacing `_sky3` (`--layerhue 1 --skysplit 1`) with `_hue` (`--layerhue 1`) keeps Band 1 (`phase === 'light'`) as 6 unified, unfragmented mop washes (`42` strokes total, `4.2%` of planned strokes) instead of splitting Band 1 into 200+ warm/cool fragments, while still preserving `--layerhue 1` separation (warm ochre/sienna, cool blue, green) in the middle wash layers.
     - Third, replacing `_lay4` (`4` middle quantile layers) with `_lay2` (`2` middle wash layers `b2L0` and `b2L1`, `33.7%` and `29.3%` of strokes) prevents the painting from stalling in middle washes past the 50% stroke mark.
4. **Added Split Soft-Edge Control (`--softsplit 1 --softmm 0.6`), Tuned Minimum Shape Floor (`_ms1500`), and Cool Gain (`_cg12`)**:
   - *Why*: `--softsplit 1 --softmm 0.6` softens turning edges with a clean damp brush right up to—without crossing—adjacent hard boundaries, preventing soft-edge bleed across crisp hull and spar edges while keeping clean-water softening strokes to 1 arc per soft passage. `_ms1500` keeps wash planes bold and cohesive while allowing `>= 12`-cell `accentCells` (`_dp85_dg2`) in Bands 4 and 5 (`16.4%` + `16.4%` of strokes, including `round4` and `rigger` darks).
5. **Rebalanced Touch-Up Rounds (`_tp1` + `--touchdmin 0.12` instead of `_tp2` at default `0.04`)**:
   - *Why*: In Try 1, `_tp2` added 1,809 strokes at the very end of the run (33% of all 5,481 strokes!), which compressed Bands 4 and 5 into the 59%–67% stroke window and left the 75%–100% window static. Using 1 selective touch-up round (`_tp1`) with `--touchdmin 0.12` keeps touch-up strokes concise so that the 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% stroke checkpoints span `b1 -> b2L0 -> b2L1 -> b4 -> b5 + tp1`.

### Critique Points Rejected
- **"No light-to-dark order / straight opacity dissolve of the finished scene"**: We reject the progression judge's claim that the painter had no light-to-dark order (darks like the hawsehole, fenders, and waterline were strictly in Bands 4–5). However, we directly fixed why the 10%, 25%, and 50% frames *appeared* like a full-sheet fade-in by removing `_lay4` and `_sky3` (`_lay2_hue`), disabling horizontal `skyLanes` (`_noslane`), and trimming the 1,809-stroke `_tp2` tail (`_tp1` + `--touchdmin 0.12`).
- **Low VLM holistic scores (`3/10`) on overall composition/paper**: Classic measures (`7.54`, edge `8.80`, value `7.05`) confirm that the warm cream paper (`221,215,199`), pure-watercolor (no-ink) approach, and deep-accent threshold (`_dp85_dg2`) work well; we kept those foundations intact.

## 3. Region Technique, Moisture, Load & Layer Order (50 Passes, 813 Shapes)
1. **Protected Lights (Before Wash)**: Connected near-paper highlights on the sunlit right hull, anchor fluke, and water glints are held as bare `221,215,199` cream paper (`--paperlights 1 --paperrel 1`, `reserves: []`, `9.3%` of sheet).
2. **Opening Drawing (`opening-lines.json`)**: `"medium": "none"`, `lines: []` (pure watercolor, no ink or graphite).
3. **Band 1 — Unified Light Foundation (`passes 1–6`, `6` shapes, `42` mop strokes, `4.2%`, `wet on wet` / `wet on dry`, water `0.62–0.88`)**: Broad `38 mm` mop sweeps establish the pale warm sunlight glow and cool shadow base across the sheet.
4. **Band 2 Layer 0 — First Hue-Separated Middle Wash (`passes 7–22`, `268` shapes, `341` strokes, `33.7%`, `wet on dry`, water `0.62`)**: Broad `25.4 mm` flat (`170` strokes), `38 mm` mop (`149` strokes), and `round12`/`round8` (`22` strokes) washes separate warm ochre/sienna timbers & anchor from cool cerulean/ultramarine hull & sap-green water reflections.
5. **Band 2 Layer 1 — Second Hue-Separated Middle Wash (`passes 23–38`, `251` shapes, `297` strokes, `29.3%`, `wet on dry`, water `0.62`)**: Covers the darker 70% of the smoothed value field (`L1`), deepening the curved bow shadow, lower-left hull shadow, diagonal spar, and water troughs, with `--softsplit 1 --softmm 0.6` damp-brush softening on turning edges.
6. **Band 4 & Band 5 — Cross-Region Shadow Glazes & Deep Accents (`passes 39–44` and `45–50`, `144` shapes / `166` strokes each, `32.8%` combined, `glazed`, water `0.45`, `--darkgain 1.3`, `--deepaccent 85 --deepgrow 2`)**: Coherent ultramarine/burnt-sienna shadow glazes (`--shadowraw 1 --violetshadow 1`) across the bow flare and waterline using `mop` and `flat1in`, plus crisp `round12`, `round8`, `round4` (`45` strokes/band), and `rigger` (`11` strokes/band) accents on the oval hawsehole, hanging fenders, anchor chain, and deep waterline base.
7. **Touchpoint Pass (`_tp1`, `--touchdmin 0.12`) & Closing (`closing-lines.json`)**: A single selective corrective glaze on shapes still lighter than the target (`d > 0.12`), with `"medium": "none"` at closing.

## 4. Failure Signs to Watch During `bench/paint.sh look`
- Any geometric or unnatural cutout in the lower-left anchor or bow shadow (confirming the Try 1 rectangular reserve bug is gone).
- Pinkish or unnatural hue cast in the early washes (confirming the 5-pigment Sargent palette replaces quinacridone rose cleanly).
- Over-darkening or loss of the sunlit cream hull and foreground anchor highlights.
