# Painting Plan — `mittens` (Try 3 of 3: Watercolor Portrait Sketch, Fine Lines and Light Washes)

## 1. Visual Diagnosis of Try 2 & Code-Level Root Causes Fixed in Try 3

In Try 2 (`6.37/10` finished, `2.8/5` progression), three specific planner and material interactions caused the visual and progression flaws flagged by both our independent review and the six-judge panel:

1. **Why Try 2 had zero middle-value passes (`b2`, `b3`) and stepped contour-map bands on the man's face**:
   - In `src/paint/methods/light-to-dark.mjs` (lines 139–148), any style profile other than `precise-watercolor` (`ink-and-wash` or `atmospheric-wet`) overwrites the target-derived 5-band weights (`light`, `middle`, smooth cubic Hermite `shadow`, `edgeFade`, `deepAccent`, `accentShare`) with a flat 2-band step function (`[0.42, 0, 0, 0.58, 0]` for `ink-and-wash`, `[0.55, 0, 0, 0.45, 0]` for `atmospheric-wet`).
   - Because `weights[1]` and `weights[2]` were zeroed out everywhere, Try 2 had **zero Band 2 or Band 3 passes** (jumping straight from 6 Band 1 passes to 39 Band 4 shadow passes) and dumped 55%–100% of non-shadow pigment into Band 1, while creating a razor-sharp step across the man's forehead and cheek at the `shadowMask` threshold.
   - **Try 3 Fix**: Set `styleSelection.style` to `"precise-watercolor"` (the identity profile used for `subject-c` in `src/paint/tuned/subjects.json`), while setting `"baseMove": "wet on wet"` on `background` and `foliage` families. This restores the full 5-band light-to-dark progression (`b1` pale foundation -> `b2L0` & `b2L1` middle form layers -> `b3` middle-dark planes -> `b4` connected shadows -> `b5` deep accents).
   - In addition, we activate `_lay2_ls35` (`--midlayers 2 --layersmooth 3.5`), `_spl8_lad_wre15_sml45` (`--strokeplan 1 --lanemm 8 --laneadapt 1 --washreach 1.5 --strokemm 45`), and `_softs` (`--softsplit 1 --softarcs 12 --softarclen 40 --softmm 0.7`) so middle-value passages are laid in pre-wetted overlapping brush lanes charged by local target demand (`PF = 0.45..1.8x`), with `1.5 mm` dilated wash reach and clean damp-brush edge softening along turning boundaries.

2. **Why Try 2's opening lines looked like "speckle or dotted trails" (named 2x at 10%)**:
   - In Try 2, `_opp22` (`--openingpressure 0.22`) clamped nib pressure `s = 0.22`, shrinking contact width to `0.173 mm` (`0.74 px`, sub-pixel) and accelerating pen speed `v = 1.9 - 1.1*s = 1.66` in `src/media/pen.mjs`. At sub-pixel contact and high speed, the nib skipped across cold-press paper hollows and deposited isolated dots on paper peaks.
   - Similarly, `--closingedgemin 0.14` and `--closingpressure 0.38` in `extra-args.json` shattered closing lines into tiny broken segments in `prepareDrawing`.
   - **Try 3 Fix**: Use `_opp68` (`--openingpressure 0.68`) and retain `_ink4`'s `--closingpressure 1 --closingedgemin 0`, with `--drawstroke 0.35` and `_sep3` (`--inkkscale 3`). Every opening line (`weights` `0.64..0.74`) now has a `~1.35 px` continuous nib footprint at steady speed, producing unbroken fine sepia sketch lines with zero dotted trails, and every closing line (`weights` `0.72..0.92`) draws continuously without edge-gate fragmentation. All opening and closing lines are clipped off `protectedCells` (`reserves`) so `selectValueInk` drops zero lines.

3. **Why Try 2 had bright ultramarine blue camo patches in the man's curly hair**:
   - `BASE` includes `_skyb` (`--skyblue 1 --skyfloc 0.5`), `_cg13` (`--coolgain 1.3`), and `_rg10` (`--groundglaze 1`). With no sky segment on `mittens`, `isSky(i)` in `paint-plate.mjs` matched every pixel with `B - R > 8` (cool darks inside the man's curly hair) and routed them to a granulating flocculating ultramarine sky-blue tube at `1.3x` gain, while `_rg10` misclassified lower-frame skin/fur as road pavement.
   - **Try 3 Fix**: Override with `_rg0` (`--groundglaze 0`), `_cg10` (`--coolgain 1.0`), `"--skyblue", "0"`, and `"--paints", "ochre,rawsienna,sienna,sapgreen,sepia,paynes"`. Using Payne's grey + sepia instead of French ultramarine gives cohesive charcoal-brown hair darks, soft blue-grey background bokeh, and crisp cat irises without electric-blue blotches.

## 2. Five-Stage Progression Design

1. **Opening Ink Sketch (`opening-lines.json`)**: ~50 continuous structural sepia lines (`_opp68`, `_sep3`) establishing both subjects' proportions, eyes, noses, mouths, cat ears, and outer contours before any wash is laid.
2. **Band 1 — Pale Local-Color Foundation (`light/b1`)**: Transparent foundations (`light = min(0.28, 0.085/v)`) preserving explicit paper reserves (`reserves` in `config.json`) on eye catchlights, cat muzzle pad, and nose bridge highlight; laid wet-on-wet in the background/foliage and wet-on-dry on the figures.
3. **Band 2 & 3 — Modulated Middle Value Planes (`mid/b2L0`, `mid/b2L1`, `mid/b3`)**: Two smoothed middle layers (`_lay2_ls35`, `_mg11`) laid in adaptive 8 mm brush lanes (`_spl8_lad_wre15_sml45`) with local per-lane pigment factors and damp-brush turning-edge softening (`_softs`), modeling the roundness of the man's cheek/forehead and cat's flame-point ears and muzzle without contour rings.
4. **Band 4 & 5 — Connected Shadows & Focal Accents (`shadow/b4`, `shadow/b5`)**: Cross-region shadow glazes (`_st38`, `_shw48`, `_dk12`, `_efade`) ramping smoothly via `0.72 * t^2 * (3 - 2t)`, followed by Band 5 deep focal accents (`_dp75`, `_acc2`) in the pupils, lash lines, nostrils, smile crease, and deepest hair curls, plus a constrained 6-family touch pass (`_tp1`).
5. **Closing Directional Sepia Ink (`closingink4-lines.json`)**: ~100 crisp, continuous sepia ink strokes (`_ink4`, `_sep3`) defining the cat's almond eye rims, slit pupils, whiskers, ear tufts, and the man's eyes, brows, smile lines, and curly hair locks.
