# Leaderboard

Generated from [results.json](results.json) by `scripts/build-leaderboard.mjs`.

Every published result: painter V096 · sim 100 · judges J1 · releases 0.1.0 and 0.1.1. V1-V3 used the models' own toolkit on this painter's simulator; V4/V5 used the V096 pipeline (unpublished benchmark build, same recipe). Each result is labelled with its round, painter version, sim version and release. To benchmark a newer painter or sim, rerun V4 or V5 with it. To reproduce a result, check out its release tag (v0.1.0, v0.1.1 and v0.1.2).

A round is a benchmark version (V1 to V5). A trial is one model session that paints the requested targets. Score is the mean over every scored painting in trials 1 to 3 of its automated score as a percentage of the target scored against itself (the realistic ceiling: Hills 8.43, France 8.43, Ships 8.76, Mittens 8.76 and Trumbull 8.57), ± the half-width of its 95% confidence interval, estimated by resampling paintings. Raw score is the same mean out of 10. Models are ranked by Score. Best trial gives the indexed score and trial number. Strokes, minutes, tokens and cost are medians per trial. Every trial the cheat-risk score flagged was reviewed by hand, and none of them cheated. One trial was disqualified for a blocked target read ([findings](FINDINGS.md#target-reads-and-cheat-risk)). Treat small differences as noise. Method: [scoring](../docs/scoring.md) and [statistics](../docs/statistics.md).

≈$ estimates what a subscription run would cost through the API: its input and output token counts times the list prices in [api-prices.json](../api-prices.json). Per-token runs show the billed cost.
