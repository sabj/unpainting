# Architecture

Settle geometry and light before surface texture. A pale wall, a lit jamb and a dark opening should already explain the building at small scale.

## Order

1. Ink walls, roofs, eaves, openings and overlaps with shared endpoints. Windows take the orientation and scale of their plane. Let the ink dry and set.
2. Reserve the broad lit plane and any important narrow border, using paper, pale paint, negative painting or masking.
3. Mix the main value groups. Paint plane-sized washes with deliberate direction before any brick, tile or foliage marks.
4. Connect washes where one shadow crosses several regions. Keep differently lit faces distinct; separately labeled areas can share a continuous wash.
5. Set each edge on its own. A crisp eave can hold softly varied paint inside its shadow.
6. Inspect the drawing and first washes before detail. If geometry or value grouping is unclear, test one opening or gable in a few tones.

## Sources

- [Toby Haseler's line-and-wash sketch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=71lPMn5MwvU&t=248s): a pen drawing first, then loose washes and a connected sidewall and roof shadow. His few late pen touches are optional; the drawing does its work before the paint.
- David Cox, [*Treatise on Landscape Painting and Effect in Water Colours*](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/65962/pg65962-images.html): [outline](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/65962/pg65962-images.html#ON_OUTLINE), [light and shade](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/65962/pg65962-images.html#ON_LIGHT_AND_SHADE_AND_EFFECT) and [laying on tints](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/65962/pg65962-images.html#METHOD_OF_LAYING_ON_THE_TINTS). The window, door and gable plates isolate structure. They are illustrations and record no painting sequence.
- [Paul Clark's Italian village](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MVyxMQi7Z7U&t=174s): broad angular placements, pale wall planes kept, wet color charges, drying, then selective detail.
- Harry Willson, [*The Use of a Box of Colours*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/32681): prepared tints and selected structural marks. His erasing methods remove more than gentle lifting and need separate simulation code for paper abrasion.

Demonstrations do not give brush pressure, water ratios or drying thresholds. Measure those in the simulator.
