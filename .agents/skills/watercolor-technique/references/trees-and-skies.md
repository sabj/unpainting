# Trees and skies

Plan connected passages and edges before texture. Distance, light and shape decide whether a passage merges softly, keeps crisp gaps or breaks across the paper tooth, the surface texture.

## Foliage and branches

[Paul Clark's tree demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ygV5PuvzjE4&t=217s) shows side contact and broken foliage marks. Its [oak study](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ygV5PuvzjE4&t=458s) builds an irregular light canopy, shadow paint added to wet washes, trunk and ground. [Oliver Pyle's tree demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wU6jjU-CwYs&t=565s) separates soft distant foliage from nearer openings and structure.

Choose the canopy's main value shape and reserve visible gaps showing sky through foliage as it grows. Charge shadows into the still-wet wash, or use separate dry-paper groups when the target shows crisp shadows. A ragged edge can surround a connected wet interior.

Use the brush's side for a broad mass and its point for structure. [Pyle's branch sequence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wU6jjU-CwYs&t=2538s) narrows the gesture by changing contact. Plan how to narrow the stroke and lift the brush, and let some branch ends disappear into foliage. Join trunk bases to the ground while that contact is still wet.

## Sky and clouds

[Pyle's sky demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pn5C1SSxucs&t=290s) joins a warm horizon mix and a cooler upper mix in one broad passage. Set the large gradient and light direction before cloud detail, and keep a wet edge across intended joins.

Choose how each cloud light survives: reserved paper, negative painting (painting around a light shape) or a lift at the right moisture. [Clark's sky demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZDnlFOgd6Fk&t=88s) lifts large upper clouds and finer lower streaks, then dries, rewets and adds soft undersides while keeping the tops bright.

[Pyle's cloud work](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pn5C1SSxucs&t=431s) varies brush size and waits for a workable moisture window. Measure paper moisture in the simulator; video elapsed time is not a drying instruction.

## Softening and lifting

[Pyle's corrections](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pn5C1SSxucs&t=1005s) separate blending an edge with a damp brush from removing pigment. Clean picked-up pigment from the brush between passes. Lifting rarely recovers paper white, so reserve essential lights early.

## Review

Check the first wash for connection, dried value, light hierarchy and edges before branches or cloud texture. Paper grain does not justify uniform texture across a smooth sky. If broad strokes leave unintended gaps, check delivered paint and contact with the paper before changing the paper settings.
