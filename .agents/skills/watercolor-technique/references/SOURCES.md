# Sources

Demonstrations give no drying time, water ratio, pressure or stroke count: measure in the simulator.

## Simulation papers

- Curtis, Anderson, Seims, Fleischer and Salesin, [*Computer-Generated Watercolor*](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/projects/watercolor/), SIGGRAPH 1997.
- Small, [*Simulating watercolor by modeling diffusion, pigment, and paper fibers*](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.44417), SPIE 1991.
- Van Laerhoven and Van Reeth, [*Real-time simulation of watery paint*](https://doi.org/10.1002/cav.95), 2005.
- Deegan et al., [*Capillary flow as the cause of ring stains from dried liquid drops*](https://doi.org/10.1038/39827), Nature 1997.
- Kubelka, [*New contributions to the optics of intensely light-scattering materials, part II*](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSA.44.000330), JOSA 1954; Haase and Meyer, [*Modeling pigmented materials for realistic image synthesis*](https://doi.org/10.1145/146443.146452), 1992.
- Strassmann, [*Hairy Brushes*](https://doi.org/10.1145/15886.15911), SIGGRAPH 1986; Baxter, Scheib, Lin and Manocha, [*DAB*](https://doi.org/10.1145/383259.383313), SIGGRAPH 2001.

## Reference implementations

- [Sudo Aquarelle](https://sudoaquarelle.com/), Lionel Mora: technique inspiration; no code is included.
- [Inkwash](https://johnowhitaker.github.io/inkwash/about), Jonathan Whitaker, and its [source](https://github.com/johnowhitaker/inkwash).

## Other references

- Lifting, masking and paper: [Winsor & Newton on lifting](https://www.winsornewton.com/blogs/guides/how-to-lift-watercolour) and [paper](https://eu.winsornewton.com/blogs/guides/best-paper-for-watercolour); [Daniel Smith on masking](https://danielsmith.com/tutorials/watercolor-masking-fluid/).
